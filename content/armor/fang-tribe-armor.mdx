## Acquiring
### Saque
Fang Basic, Medium, Advanced, and Winter pieces are found on defeated or deterred members of the Fang Tribe. The Winter set is only found on Fang Tribesmen living in snowy conditions. The Everyday set cannot be found as drops, but Pode ser fabricado at an Armor Forging Table.

Looted versions of the armor pieces cannot be repaired at a Repair Bench.
### Crafting
Initially, none of the pieces Pode ser fabricado. A Master Craftsman of the tribe must be successfully deterred to unlock the Costume, Pants, Gloves, and Shoes of one of the class sets. This must be done 5 times, one for each class.
