## Set Bonuses
Wearing two or more pieces from the same set grants additional, cumulative effects:

### 
*2 pieces - Poison-proof: 20%
*4 pieces - STA-Down DEF: 20%, Max Load: 40
*6 pieces - Max Resilience: 50, Heat-proof: 25, DEF: 30

### Advanced
*2 pieces - Poison-proof: 25%
*4 pieces - STA-Down DEF: 25%, Max Load: 50
*6 pieces - Max Resilience: 75, Heat-proof: 30, DEF: 45


Wearing the full 6-piece set grants an additional 50% Poison-proofing and cleanses -3 Body Poison per minute.

Combining the regular and Advanced sets will not grant cross-bonus or full-set effects. They must be from the same tier.
