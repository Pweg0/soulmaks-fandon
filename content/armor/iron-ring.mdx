# Iron Ring

<br/><br/><br/>






}}
**Iron Ring** is an armor accessory in *Soulmask*.

A ring made of iron.

Base stats:

* Max HP: 120

* ATK: 20

* Max HP: 4%
* ATK: 4%

* Arm Resilience: 165

* Durability: 3000


A variant called "Worn " can be found in chests and looted off slain  enemies. This version has visible cracks compared to the craftable , and cannot be repaired. It has the same stats, but lower durability.
