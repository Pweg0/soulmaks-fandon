## Set Bonuses
Wearing two or more pieces from the same set grants additional, cumulative effects:

### 
*2 pieces - Max Resilience: 40
*4 pieces - Crit DMG Reduction: 20%, Max Load: 60
*6 pieces - DMG Reduction: 10%, Chill-proof: 30, DEF: 60

### Advanced
*2 pieces - Max Resilience: 50
*4 pieces - Crit DMG Reduction: 24%, Max Load: 90
*6 pieces - DMG Reduction: 15%, Chill-proof: 36, DEF: 90


Wearing the full 6-piece set grants an additional 15% Crit and 15% Crit Resist.

Combining the regular and Advanced sets will not grant cross-bonus or full-set effects. They must be from the same tier.
