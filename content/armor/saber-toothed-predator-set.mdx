## Set Bonuses
Wearing two or more pieces from the same set grants additional, cumulative effects:

### 
*2 pieces - Max Resilience: 20
*4 pieces - Crit: 10%, Max Load: 40
*6 pieces - ATK: 5%, Concealment: 20, Sound Coefficient: -20%

### Advanced
*2 pieces - Max Resilience: 25
*4 pieces - Crit: 12%, Max Load: 50
*6 pieces - ATK: 7.5%, Concealment: 30, Sound Coefficient: -24%

Wearing the full 6-piece set grants an additional 5% Attack Speed and 50% faster Bleed Trigger Rate.

Combining the regular and Advanced sets will not grant cross-bonus or full-set effects. They must be from the same tier.
