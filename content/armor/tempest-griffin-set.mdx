## Set Bonuses
Wearing two or more pieces from the same set grants additional, cumulative effects:

### 
*2 pieces - Radiation-proof: 20%
*4 pieces - Crit DMG Increase: 20%, Movement SPD: 5%
*6 pieces - ATK: 5%, Max Load: 100, STA-Down DEF: 10%

### Advanced
*2 pieces - Radiation-proof: 25%
*4 pieces - Crit DMG Increase: 24%, Movement SPD: 6%
*6 pieces - ATK: 7.5%, Max Load: 150, STA-Down DEF: 12%

Wearing the full 6-piece set grants an additional 50% Radiation-proofing and cleanses -3 Body Radiation per minute.

Combining the regular and Advanced sets will not grant cross-bonus or full-set effects. They must be from the same tier.
