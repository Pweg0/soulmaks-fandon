## Set Bonuses
Wearing two or more pieces from the same set grants additional, cumulative effects:

### 
*2 pieces - Stamina Recovery: 15
*4 pieces - Max Resilience: 24, Max Load: 60
*6 pieces - Max HP: 5%, DEF: 40, Crit DMG Increase: 40%

### Advanced
*2 pieces - Stamina Recovery: 20
*4 pieces - Max Resilience: 36, Max Load: 80
*6 pieces - Max HP: 7.5%, DEF: 60, Crit DMG Increase: 48%

Wearing the full 6-piece set grants an additional +50 Stamina and +0.5% HP every second.

Combining the regular and Advanced sets will not grant cross-bonus or full-set effects. They must be from the same tier.
