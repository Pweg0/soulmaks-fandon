**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Visão Geral
 is the second variant of the Fogfrog. It can be summoned in front of the Hill Pyramid in the Volcanic Forest region by crafting its token at the central altar.

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Fogfrog Mark and a Fogfrog Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Berserk Fogfrog Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Fogfrog Statue
* Fogfrog Set, which includes blueprints for:
**Frog Leather Armor
**Frog Leather Pants
**Frog Leather Boots
**Frog Claw Gloves
**Frog Bone Necklace
**Frog Bone Ring
