**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Visão Geral
 is the second variant of the Vajra Ape. It can be summoned in front of the Table Mountain Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Vajra Ape Mark, Vajra Ape Fang, 10 blue crystals (2 people), 24 green crystals, and a Vajra Ape Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion,  Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Vajra Ape Statue
*Giant Ape Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Giant Ape Leather Armor
**Giant Ape Leather Pants
**Giant Ape Leather Boots
**Giant Ape Gloves
**Giant Ape Necklace
**Giant Ape Ring
