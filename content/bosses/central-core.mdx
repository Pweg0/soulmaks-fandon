## Summoning the Central Core
thumbBefore being summoned,  sits in the center of the room under a blue pyramid-shaped shield. The Core appears like a pyramid made of haphazardly-placed metal bricks.

The shield encompasses most of the room, and is impenetrable until you activate each of the 5 narrow pyramid devices (they look like Radiation Towers). One is directly in front where you enter the room, two on the left side, and two on the right. They require the Mysterious Devices Encontrado em each of the 5 Pyramids that till now had no purpose. 

Placing one will cause its pedestal to shoot a blue laser toward the ceiling, where the inverted ceiling pyramid meets the shield. The shield will drop after the final device is activated, and Central Core will "wake up" and attack almost immediately.
## Combat Abilities
 has two phases: Blue and Red. The phases are not connected to its health, but to time spent fighting it. It swaps from one to the other repeatedly until defeated. There is a long pause between each phase where the Core retreats back to its 'haphazard bricks' look, and a shorter pause between the attacks within each phase. These pauses are ideal times to attack it with impunity.
### Blue Phase
thumb
Blue phase attacks are a bit reminiscent of Mechanical Core inimigos, in that its primary weapons are lasers it shoots from its core and corners.
***Stationary single laser:** One laser beam shoots from the center of one side. It tracks o jogador, but is easily evaded by running to another side, since the beam cannot move past a certain angle.
***Stationary quadruple laser:** Four laser beams shoot from the center of all four sides. Can be dodged simply by moving aside, as the lasers don't move after firing. Can also be ducked under when in melee range, since the Core's height when floating is higher than most character's heads.
***Rotating quadruple laser:** Four laser beams shoot from the bottom corners of 's pyramid as it rotates (slightly faster than running-speed). o jogador can run and roll in the same direction as the rotation to stay between the lasers, or jump over them as they pass.
***Room-wide multi-laser sweep:** The Core will float up and turn upside down, then shoot a wide laser down to the middle of the floor. The beam slowly widens into a multiple-laser radial sweep that moves outward and up to the ceiling, then back down to the center. While nearly impossible to avoid from melee range, at a distance there is enough room between the laser beams to stand still and not be hit.
### Red Phase
thumb
***Lightning ground stomp:** The Core will rise up, then smash down on the ground, releasing a circular lightning attack that radiates outward to the edges of the arena. 
***Room-wide lightning strike:** The Core will gather lightning at its peak and strike all over the room in random spots. It does this twice.
***Lightning 'mines':** Red circles will appear on the ground, followed by a red lightning strike on that area after a short delay. The circles remain stationary where they appear, so they can be moved away from if quick enough. They will repeatedly appear wherever a player is standing however, so you will want to be moving constantly during this attack.
***Slow-debuff bricks:** The Core will summon floating red cubes and silver rectangular bricks around it, which will float toward jogadores. Getting hit by one applies a slow debuff that *drastically* affects movement speed. The bricks move slowly, so they can be avoided by running away. They will eventually despawn or be destroyed by the Core's other attacks.
::Notes: 
::*The debuff doesn't show up on the top right as a status effect, but it affects o jogador like one.
::*The debuff stacks, which means getting hit by multiple bricks can bring your entire movement to a halt as if the character was impossibly over-encumbered.
::*thumb|Minions being summonedThis is probably the attack that will bring the fight to an end for most jogadores, because while you can dodge roll through it, there is no way to actually remove the debuff until it wears off.
***Minion summons:** One or two lightning-ringed black holes will open up next to the Core, and either a Slayer or Mechanical Core will drop out of each. These inimigos can be dispatched the same way you normally would, or you can avoid them and they will either despawn after long enough, or be killed by the Core's other attacks.

## Rewards
The primary rewards for defeating the  are access to Ancient Enhance Table technology, a Lighting Module, a Trip Milestone/achievement ("Central Core"), and bragging rights. Its actual death package loot is underwhelming for the scope of the fight (hopefully this will change), and consists of:
*Mysterious Metal Chunk
*Metal Plate
*Pedra Solar (Enough energy)
*Cristal Verde
*A random legendary quality Cristal Vermelho Mask mod
*Energy Module
*Mysterious Alloy
In the room behind it however, there are two Ancient Relic chests containing rare enhancement-related crafting materials, Heaven Worship Containers, and Ancient Texts, which can be read to unlock the Ancient Enhance Table technology.
### Lighting Module
thumb|Lighting ModuleAfter the Core is defeated, a tiny version of it will be left floating at the center of the room. Approaching it and pressing E will slot the module into your mask, and a "Mini Central Core" will start to follow you around like a familiar. It has an extremely bright, very wide radius of light, and emits a trail of light particles that can be seen from behind (but only by the user). The light flood penetrates floors and walls, and is *very* easily seen by far away jogadores, so it is not recommended to use on a PvP server.

The Mini Core will follow o jogador until it is manually turned off, which can be done via the Mask Node Repair screen (Y). Beneath the icon of the mask will be a new pyramid icon. Click it to summon or de-summon the Lighting Module.

A player's access to a slotted Lighting Module is permanent for that world, and the toggle button transfers between masks.
## Notas
*About one minute after the fight begins, the shield will descend again, trapping everyone inside until the fight is over. If planning to take on the  in a party, make sure nobody remains near the walls of the room, or they will be locked out of the fight. 
*The Core's Blue Phase laser attacks cannot penetrate the shield, but many of its Red Phase lightning attacks do. Having an AFK character stand closer to the walls before the fight will not ensure their safety.
*Logging out after a fight is complete and then logging back into the Central Core chamber risks auto-restarting the fight with your character locked either outside or within the shield with the Core activated, which means the exit door will no longer be accessible. The shield can be lowered again by re-activating the Mysterious Device pedestals, or you can leave the room the way you came so the Core resets. If stuck inside, the Core (or o jogador) must be defeated again to drop the shield unless someone else can deactivate it from the outside.
*Trying to open the door while the Core is alive will show the message "Danger detected nearby. Unable to unlock."
*The Lighting Module's light glows very fluorescent blue-white (over 4000 on the Kelvin color scale), so jogadores looking to keep the warmer oranges and yellows of the firelight/primitive ambiance may wish to avoid using it in their base.
