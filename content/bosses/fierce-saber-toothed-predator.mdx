**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Visão Geral
 is the first variant of the Saber-toothed Predator. It can be summoned in front of the Rainforest Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Fight
The boss has high mobility and fast attacks that apply bleed stacks. It will attack and roar almost immediately after being summoned, and its tactics involve running around the arena and jumping at jogadores to attack them before quickly retreating. Do not ever keep your back turned to this boss, as it has very fast attacks and moves around the arena a lot.  

### Recommendations
It is recommended to have at least Beast Bone Weapons and Beast Hide Gear before attempting this boss. Dual-blades, Spear, or Gauntlets are ideal for fast damage, however it is doable with any weapon. Simple Bandages or Premium Bandages are important to regenerate lost health and remove bleeding stacks. 

### Melee
This tactic will give you the quickest and highest damage in a short amount of time, as the fight will drag on if you keep your distance. 
*Start the fight with a long rage weapon spear or bow to avoid the initial roar. Keep your distance initially, as the roar has a large AOE. 
*This boss has two phases. A slow stalk and a sprint.
*Slow stalk phase: Walk towards the boss to force it to jump back and then pounce. To avoid the pounce, run or dodge forward slightly to the left or right depending on which side the boss jumps back from. The goal is to run or roll underneath the boss while it is mid-pounce, so you end up behind it.
*Sprint phase: Walk towards it and follow the direction it sprints in. Its eyes will glow red as a cue before it pounces. After the pounce the boss will strike three times in front of it, which should allow you to turn around and hit it from behind, getting in one to two good hits.
You do not need to sprint at all during the fight, except to get back to the boss after a pounce. Otherwise you will waste stamina needed for dodge rolls and attacks. If you get hit, apply a bandage only immediately after successfully dodging a pounce, as this is the only decent window of time to do so.

If your melee damage is not high enough, try to keep a distance, dodge roll whenever possible, and use ranged attacks.

## Notas
*Saber-toothed Predator will lose aggro and start regenerating health if it goes too far from the arena.
*This boss is the easiest way to farm Cristal Azuls, which are needed in mid-to-late game.
*Saber-toothed Predator's teeth can be broken with Hammer and they will be on the ground as extra lootable (extra fangs).
