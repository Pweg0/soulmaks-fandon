**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Visão Geral
 is the third variant of the Fogfrog. It can be summoned in front of the Hill Pyramid in the Volcanic Forest region by crafting its token at the central altar.

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Fogfrog Mark and a Fogfrog Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Fogfrog of Doom Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Golden Fogfrog Statue
*Advanced Fogfrog Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Advanced Frog Leather Armor
**Advanced Frog Leather Pants
**Advanced Frog Leather Boots
**Advanced Frog Claw Gloves
**Advanced Frog Bone Necklace
**Advanced Frog Bone Ring
