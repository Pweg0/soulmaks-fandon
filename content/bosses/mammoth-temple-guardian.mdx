# Mammoth (Temple Guardian)

**Mammoth** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

It can be summoned at the Snow Mountain Pyramid in the Alpine Land region.<br>
400px

There are three variants, each increasing in difficulty:
*Titan Mammoth
*Berserk Mammoth
*Mammoth of Doom
