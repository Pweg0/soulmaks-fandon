**Poison Fogfrog** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Overview
Poison Fogfrog is the first variant of the Fogfrog. It can be summoned in front of the Hill Pyramid in the Volcanic Forest region by crafting its token at the central altar.

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Fogfrog Mark and a Fogfrog Key. If the player has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Poison Fogfrog Head can also be harvested from it.

## Fight
The boss has a low amount of HP, but it is located in a poisonous area full of Arrow-Poison Frogs. It also shoots out clouds of poisonous gas that must be avoided.

## Tips
The Nature Enlightenment Mask's passive effect of making non-elite animals ignore the wearer comes in very useful here, as you can completely ignore the Arrow-Poison Frogs in the arena that would normally be frustrating obstacles during the fight.

You can also kill all the Arrow-Poison Frogs around arena before summoning to prevent them getting in the way while you fight.
