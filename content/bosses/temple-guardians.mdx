# Temple Guardians

**** are boss creatures in *Soulmask.*
## Overview
Each  of the map's five Pyramids has a formidable creature guarding the valuable knowledge inside.

## Summoning
Temple Guardians can be summoned at their respective Pyramids by crafting tokens at their altars. Tokens require Worship Containers (dropped from high ranking members of hostile tribes) combined with various other materials. See specific Guardian pages for details on what summoning materials are needed. 
:Note: Tokens are not technically inventory items, as they are consumed immediately upon crafting.

Each guardian has 3 tiers, progressing in difficulty, power and rewards. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drop Keys, Marks, crafting materials, and rare items, including unique heads that can be turned into display specimens. Berserk and Doom Guardians can also unlock special armor sets and statues usable as base decorations.
