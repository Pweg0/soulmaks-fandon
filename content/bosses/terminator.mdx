# Terminator

}}
**** is a boss inimigo in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
 is a powerful robotic inimigo and second boss Encontrado em Ancient Dungeons. It guards the final room which contains an Ancient Hero's Coffin, a Golden Relic Chest, and the return portal.
## Aparência
When dormant,  looks like a heap of scrap metal. When sensing o jogador, the parts float up and arrange into a large 6-legged, 4-armed Slayer-type robot with multiple weapons and attack styles. Its two upper arms float above it and have a wider range of motion.

Like the more common Slayer and Slayer S/X robotic inimigos, Terminator's arms end in weapons. However instead of a single type, each arm ends in a different weapon: Hammer, Great Sword, Spear, and Blade. 
## Combat Abilities
### Regular Attacks
*Spin attack (similar to Slayer S/X)
*Blade slash / projectile launch (Similar to Slayer S/X)
*Spear jab / flurry attack
*Hammer blow
*Great Sword slash
### Special Attacks
*Hammer arm enlarges into a gigantic hammer that winds up and smashes the ground for a much larger AOE attack
*Great Sword arm enlarges into a gigantic sword that winds up and slashes in a very wide arc in front of it

## Strategies for Defeating
Equip appropriately-leveled gear, a good Hammer (it is immune to blade and piercing weapons) and Corrosion Tanks. Many of Terminator's attacks also add multiple stacks of Bleeding Damage, so bring appropriate food and healing items with you.

Tactics: 
*Terminator's attacks hit hard. But the time between its attacks, including the attack warmup animations, are pretty slow, so it's possible to move around and attack from behind or retreat when needed. Dodge rolling can come in handy, but considering how stop-and-go the attacks are and how much they are telegraphed beforehand, you'll have plenty of time to run away.
*The main attacks to watch out for are the spin attack, blade launch, and the enlarged weapons, which have a very large AOE/range. 
*The spear flurry attack can cause a lot of damage if you get stuck in it, so keep an eye out for when it prepares to attack with that arm and run/dodge away.
