## Usage
A Tribesman assigned to an  will run to it to use for ranged defense when needed. They can be directed to attack animals, Barbarians, enemies like scouts and invasion hordes, or all of the above.

An  can be set for "permanent defense", meaning the assigned tribesman will stay in the tower at all times (make sure they have adequate food, water, and ammo).

It has 60 (6x10) slots for storing arrows or other items, which the assigned tribesman will use while on duty.
