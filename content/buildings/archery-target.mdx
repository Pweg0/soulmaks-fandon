**Archery Target** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
A perfect opportunity to practice your aiming skills. Learned through Furniture (Technology), in the Medium Building (Technology) category in tier 1 of the Bonfire Era.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 10x Log
* 5x Branch
* 20x Thick Rope
