# Arena

**Arena** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
The arena is a place where you can set your tribesmen into fight each other, letting them prove their worthiness to be your follower. Learned through Arena (techonology) in the Advanced Building (technology) - tier 3 of the Bronze Pit Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 200x Stone Brick
* 40x Hardwood Plank
* 20x Hardwood
* 40x Thick Rope
