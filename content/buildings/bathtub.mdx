**Bathtub** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A warm refreshing bathtub for you, your tribesmen and even some pets.  Learned from Furniture (II) (Technology), in Advanced Building (Technology) - tier 3, Bronze Pit Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 30x Hardwood Plank
* 20x Stone Brick
* 20x Clay
* 10x Bronze Ingot
