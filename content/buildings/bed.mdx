**Bed** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A simple bed for one; useful for resting to restore morale.  Learned from Basic Carpentry Technique (Technology) in Basic Carpentry (Technology) - tier 2, Campfire era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Hand Crafting
* 20x Thatch
* 4x Thick Rope
* 10x Plank or Hardwood Plank or Premium Hardwood Plank
