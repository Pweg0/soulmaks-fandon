# Beehive

}}
**Beehive** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
The Beehives produce honey and bee wax. Honey is necessary for the healthy growth of certain crops in farmland and for certain cooking recipes. Bee wax is Usado para radiation mods.

To attract bees, place Bee-drawing Syrup inside. They will consume it until the hive is full enough to produce honey. After consuming 100 Bee-drawing Syrup (50 bees), the bees will become self-replicating and no longer require syrup.

It is learned with Premium Breeding , tier 3 of the Bronze Pit Bonfire

## Obtenção
Can be constructed through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 40x Plank
* 5x Thin Rope
* 20x Thatch
* 40x Wooden Strip
*

## Notas
An active beehive gives a +20% output bonus to nearby farmland.
