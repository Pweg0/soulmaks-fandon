**Blackstone Barrier** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Sturdy, blackstone walls for defending tough areas. Learned with Blackstone Wall (Technology) in Blackstone Building (Technology) - tier 2, Steel Pit Bonfire Era

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 40x Blackstone Brick
* 40x Plaster
