**Blackstone Door Frame** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A sturdy door frame made of Blackstone for the most beautiful and secure structures. Learned from Blackstone Door (Technology) in Blackstone Building (Technology) - tier 2, Steel Pit Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 4x Blackstone Brick
* 4x Plaster
