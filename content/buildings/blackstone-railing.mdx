# Blackstone Railing

\}\}
**Blackstone Railing**, also known as **Blackstone Fence**, is a building item in *Soulmask*.
## Obtenção
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Steel Pit Bonfire: Blackstone Building.
## Variantes
 has two other variants, which can be accessed by holding Z:
:60px Blackstone Stair Guardrail
:60px Blackstone Half-height Stair Guardrail
Unlike the default  which can be placed at the edge of any foundation or flooring, guardrail pieces can only be placed on the sides of stairs or half-height stairs.
## Notas
In the Knowledge and Technology tree and in the the Building Workshop crafting menu, the default item is called "Blackstone Railing". Once in the inventory and when opening the Z menu to choose variants, it is called "Blackstone Fence".
