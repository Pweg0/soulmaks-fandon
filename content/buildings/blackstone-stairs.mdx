# Blackstone Stairs

<br/>






}}
**Blackstone Stairs** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Acquisition
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Steel Pit Bonfire: Blackstone Building.
## Variants
 has several variants, which can be accessed by holding Z and choosing the form to use:
:60px Blackstone Stair Angle 
:60px Blackstone Half-height Stairs 
:60px Blackstone Half-height Stair Angle
:60px Blackstone Left-revolving Stairs
:60px Blackstone Right-revolving Stairs
## Notes
*The Blackstone Stair Angle, Blackstone Half-height Stairs, and Blackstone Half-height Stair Angle items show Blackstone Roof icons in the Z menu, but placing them still places the correct Stair item.
