**Blackstone Valve Trap** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A valve trap made of Blackstone for the most beautiful and secure structures. Learned from Guard Facility (Technology) in Blackstone Building (Technology) - tier 2, Steel Pit Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 5x Steel Ingot
* 5x Premium Hardwood Plank
