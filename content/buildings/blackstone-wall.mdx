# Blackstone Wall

<br/>






}}
**Blackstone Wall** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Acquisition
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Steel Pit Bonfire: Blackstone Building.
## Variants
 has a few variants, which can be accessed by holding Z and choosing the form to use:
:60px Blackstone Window Wall
:60px Blackstone Door Frame
:60px Blackstone Double Door Frame
:60px Blackstone Half Wall
:60px Blackstone Sloping Wall
:60px Blackstone Half-height Sloping Wall
:60px Blackstone Inverted Sloping Wall
:60px Blackstone Inverted Half-height Sloping Wall
### Notes
*Any tier of Door, Double Door, or Window piece can be placed in the respective Blackstone Door Frame, Blackstone Double Door Frame, or Blackstone Window Wall to become an openable door or window.
*Retrieving Blackstone Door Frame and Blackstone Double Door Frame will place that item in the inventory in that form. They can be reverted to any of the other forms during placement.
*Retrieving any of Blackstone Wall's other variants will return a plain Blackstone Wall to the inventory.
