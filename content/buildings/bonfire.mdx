**Bonfire** is a building and gameplay mechanic in *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
Much more than just an upgraded Campfire, a Bonfire is the gathering place at the core of a settlement, and represents the latest-achieved technology level for your tribe.
## Obtenção
The Bonfire technology in the "Dawn of the New Tribe" era on the Knowledge and Technology tree becomes available to unlock once o jogador reaches Awareness Strength 5 with their starter mask.
## Uso
Placing a Bonfire creates a circular area within which buildings are safe from decay as long as the Bonfire is lit. Build a roof over it to keep the rain at bay.

Holding E while looking at a Bonfire opens the following options:
***Open Craft:** Opens the crafting menu for adding fuel and cooking items
***Extinguish / Dismantle / Relocate / Retrieve**
***Upgrade:** As with other building items, a Bonfire can be upgraded in place if a higher-tier item is in o jogador's inventory.
***Remodel Initial Character:** If o jogador dies far from the base with their starting character, or is controlling a tribesman and their initial character is killed, this option can be chosen to respawn the starting body. Has no effect if the starting character is alive.
***Assign works to tribesmen:** Opens a menu with all tribesmen listed, where specific actions and behaviors can be assigned to each individually. 
***Rename:** Renames the Bonfire so it will show up as a different name on the HUD and World Map.
***Show Area:** Toggles a green wall showing the range that the bonfire can protect building items from decay.
*Set acceptable fuel types: Allow or disallow specific items to be used as fuel

### Benefits
Aside from protecting buildings, Bonfires give the following benefits:
*Adds the **Sweet Home** buff, which:
**Pacifies dangerous creatures in the area
**Boosts recovery, lowering the time needed to heal from injuries
**Reduces food consumption
**Slightly increases elemental resistances
*Adds the **Comfort Level** buff, which puts characters in a Pleasant Mood if they sit in a Chair, Bed, or Bathtub.
*Farmland can be placed within its range, up to a limit of 12 plots at the lowest level of Bonfire. To place more, the Bonfire must be upgraded. If two or more Bonfires overlap, both must be upgraded to increase the Farmland plot limit.
### Technology
Having a Bonfire in the camp makes the following technologies available to unlock with tech points:
*Medium Carpentry → Medium Building
*Beast Bone Tools → Medium Craftsmanship
*Medium Slaughtering
*Premium Potting → Mask Technique
*Premium Food → Drying Technique
*Animal Breeding → Crop Planting
*Kiln Technique
*Beast Bone Weapon
*Leather Gear

## Defesa
During Invasions, attacking tribes will make a beeline toward the nearest Bonfire if possible. So it is recommended to have battle-tested tribesmen geared up and ready to defend at a moment's notice while they do their other jobs.
