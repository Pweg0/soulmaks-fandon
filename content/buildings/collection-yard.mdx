# Collection Yard

<br/><br/><br/>






}}
**Collection Yard** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.


The Collection Yard works by assigning a Tribesman as a caretaker. There can be only a single tribesman appointed as a caretaker at a time. Once a Tribesman is assigned as a caretaker, they will have a "Workshop Collection" task under "Jobs", and you can filter what items to collect within the radius of the Collection Yard.
