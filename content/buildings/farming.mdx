# Farming

**Farming** is a key method of producing food, medicine, and other products and materials for your tribe in *Soulmask*. Remember: a tribe runs on its stomach!  
## Overview
Farming crops and keeping livestock requires several types of structures to build, maintain, and staff with tribesmen to keep running.

The primary structures and items involved in crop farming include:
*Farmland: A large plot of arable land that can be earmarked for a specific crop. When kept stocked with fertilizer and water and assigned a caretaker via a nearby Granary, a placed piece of farmland can grow anywhere between 9 to 25 plants, depending on the size of the grown plant (e.g. Papaya trees take up more room than Aloe bushes)
*Granary: The hub for crop farming, the Granary is where seeds, compost, and herbicides are stored. Tribesmen assigned to a Granary will automatically operate nearby farmland. Seeds will be taken from it to be planted, and harvested crops will be stored in it as long as there is room.
*Fertilizer Bucket: A workstation where various types of fertilizer and compost can be crafted
*Beehive: Some plants thrive better when pollinated by nearby bees.
*Seeds: Seeds can be crafted from crops by hand or at a Grinder, or looted from enemy farmland plots at Barbarian Barracks and Barbarian Fortresses.

For livestock and other animal-based harvesting, a tribe will need
*Breeding Farm: To maintain a meat-based food source, you will need to keep a steady supply of animals. Using Breeding Farms ensures you will not have to continually go beyond safe grounds to keep your tribe fed.
*Capybara Pen, Turkey Coop, Piranha Pool, and Barn: Safe places to keep and raise Capybaras, Turkeys, Piranhas, and mountable creatures like Alpacas and Llamas
*Monkey Storage Nest: Pet Monkeys will gather wild fruits and nuts at their nests, which can be collected and stored by tribesmen.
*Parrot Rack: Pet Scarlet Macaws act as an alarm system for intruders nearby, and can even learn a handful of phrases to repeat.

## Farming
### Farmland: Types of Crops
Growing crops in Farmland requires seeds, fertilizers, and sometimes bees for pollination.


### Compost and Fertilizers
Fertilizers are produced in the Fertilizer Bucket from feces and other components.  Livestock (Capybara Pen, Turkey Coop, etc) and the Outhouse are the main sources of feces.



### <u>Beekeeping</u>
Beehives are critical for growing certain trees in your farms, and producing Honey and Bee Wax for cooking and crafting.  Bees thrive on a diet of Bee-drawing Syrup made from Guava, Banana, Pineapple, and Papaya at the Brewing Stand.



## <u>Animal Domestication</u>
Certain creatures may be captured, domesticated, and even bred. Tribesmen can automatically manage nearby pens and coops when assigned to a Breeding Farm.

Keeping domesticated creatures has a wide range of benefits:
*Capybaras and Turkeys will breed in captivity as long as they are provided adequate food and water.
*Capybara Pens, Turkey Coops, and Piranha Pools are a good source of Fresh Meat and Fish for cooking, provided a tribesman with a butcher's knife is assigned to them. 
*Turkey Coops provide eggs and feathers in addition to meat. 
*Monkeys will gather fruits and nuts from the local environment and deposit them in Monkey Storage Nests.
*Scarlet Macaws and Monkeys can ride on players' shoulders. When used this way Macaws can act as warning systems, and Monkeys can help loot items. 
*Alpacas, Llamas, Ostriches, Jaguars, and Snow Leopards become mounts when equipped with saddles.
*All domesticated animals are a source of feces that can be collected for crafting fertilizers
