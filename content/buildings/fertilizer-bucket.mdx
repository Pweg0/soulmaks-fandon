# Fertilizer Bucket

<br/><br/>






}}
**Fertilizer Bucket** is a building in *Soulmask*.

The Fertilizer Bucket allows production of fertilizers for farming, including Compost, Ash Fertilizer, Bone Powder Fertilizer, Stone Powder Fertilizer, and Compound Fertilizer.
