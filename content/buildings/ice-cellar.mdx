# Ice Cellar

\}\}
**Ice Cellar** is a storage item in *Soulmask*.

Using Ice as fuel, Ice Cellars can greatly extend the shelf life of any food item that has a decay timer.

It has 36 (6x6) slots and can only store Food items.
