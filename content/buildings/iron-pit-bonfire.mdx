**Iron Pit Bonfire** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

The amount of farmland you can get from a low-level campfire is limited to 12. If you need more farmland, you must upgrade your campfire. If you have more than 1 campfire in your territory, the remaining campfires must also be upgraded to the same level, otherwise you will only get 12 farmland.
