# Mammoth Statue

**Mammoth Statue** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
You will be able to craft this statue after defeating the Berserk version of Mammoth.  The Technology is listed under Mammoth statue (Techonology) in Statue (Technology) - Tier 3 of the Steel Pit Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 25x Stone Brick
* 2x Steel Chisel
