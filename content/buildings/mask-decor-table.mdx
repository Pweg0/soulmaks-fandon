## Description
When you lead a tribe, you want to stand out. With the , you can do that by adding flamboyant feathers, gold elements, jewels, and other adornments to express your individual tastes and make sure you are recognized from afar.
## Acquisition
The  becomes available to unlock once the player builds a Bonfire and reaches the "Dawn of a New Tribe" era in the Knowledge and Technology tree. Its prerequisite technologies are Potting Technique and Premium Potting.
## Usage
The  has 3 functions:
*Mask Decoration: Change the colors and add or remove features of unlocked masks
*Hairstyle: Change the currently-controlled character's hairstyle
*Painting: Change the currently-controlled character's body paint
Changing any feature other than hair requires one or more colors of paint, which can be crafted at a Dye Vat.
## Mask Decoration
The Mask Decoration tab is where you can unlock and adjust various features of any masks you have access to. It allows you to:
*Change the color of a mask
*Add or remove a Feather Accessory
*Add or remove Ornaments (embellishments at the cheeks/temples)
*Change feather and ornament colors
*Craft special vanity masks
There is also a fourth Inventory tab with 36 (6x6) slots for storing materials.

Any feature, ornament, or special mask is permanently unlocked once crafted, and can be customized at will by any character you control, assuing you have the necessary paint colors handy.

Mask accessories and ornaments use a *lot* of valuable materials, so plan carefully!
## Accessories and Ornaments
Any Mask's accessory and ornament can be crafted before finding the mask itself, but it cannot be colored or customized until that mask is unlocked in the Mysterious Stone Table.

Colors shown for feathers and other elements are the default colors in the table. They can all be changed.

The Explorer Mask has no  adornments.
## Special Masks
Special Masks are cosmetic only, and can be worn "over" any other mask to replace it visually. Their colors or features cannot be changed, unlike the other mask decorations. 

They can be crafted after obtaining the heads of Temple Guardians. The "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion needs to be reached before being able to harvest creature heads.


## Hairstyle
The Hairstyle tab currently only has about 5 or 6 styles per gender at the moment. More will likely be added as game development progresses.
## Painting
The Painting tab is where the currently-controlled character's body paint can be changed. 

Initially there will be few or no options available. But with every tribesman you recruit, you add their worn body paint designs to the Decor Table. 

Four parts of the body can be painted with separate designs or colors:
*Head (Face and jaw area)
*Chest (Torso, including back)
*Arms
*Legs
