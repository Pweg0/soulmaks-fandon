# Medium Trap

}}
**Medium Trap** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Place in areas with animal activity with an appropriate bait to capture medium-sized beasts for slaughter, or their young for domestication. Learned from Trap (Technology) in Animal Breeding (Technology) - Tier 1 of the Bonfire Era.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 15x Hardwood
* 5x Plank
* 20x Thatch
* 5x Thick Rope
