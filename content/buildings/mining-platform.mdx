# Mining Platform

Mining platform is a building located in mines.
It allows extraction of iron and related minerals.

Platform can be repaired using 36 Iron Ingot, 18 Leather Rope, 18 Premium Hardwood Plank, 18 Hardwood Part.
After the repair, platform changes its affiliation to the clan of the one who repaired it.

If platform belongs to any clan, then NPCs stop appearing in the location.

Player or a designated tribesman can extract minerals from platform.
Platform needs fuel to work: Hardwood Part, Metal Part, Metal Plate, Precision Part.

Mine Chest can be installed next to platform.
