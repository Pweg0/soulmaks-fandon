# Monkey Storage Nest

<br/><br/>







}}
**Monkey Storage Nest** is a building in *Soulmask*.


Pet Monkeys will gather fruits and nuts from nearby trees and store them in the Monkey Storage Nest.  These nests can be an important part of the farming process.
