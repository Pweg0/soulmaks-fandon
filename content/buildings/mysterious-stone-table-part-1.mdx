## Usage
 can only be placed next to an existing Mysterious Stone Table. 

It upgrades the table to add a Remodel tab, which allows saving Tribesmen data. Saved Tribesmen can be "remodeled" after death, resurrecting them after a timer counts down (default 15 minutes, but can be adjusted down to 3 in settings). The table part will open and the newly resurrected Tribesman will step out of it.
