# Oil Lamp

**Oil Lamp** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Casts light in an area around the lamp. Improves comfort.  Learned from Advanced Furniture (Technology) in Blackstone Building (Technology) - tier 2 of Steel Pit Bonfire Era.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 2x Copper Ingot
* 5x Iron Ingot
