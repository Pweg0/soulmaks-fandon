# Oil Press

}}
**Oil Press** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Converts various ingredients into cooking oil.  Learned with Oil Press (Technology) in Oil Press Technique (Technology) - tier 2, Bronze Pit Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 20x Bronze Ingot
* 20x Hardwood Strip
* 20x Hardwood Plank
* 20x Thin Rope
