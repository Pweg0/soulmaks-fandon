**Oil Press** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Converts various ingredients into cooking oil.  Learned with Oil Press (Technology) in Oil Press Technique (Technology) - tier 2, Bronze Pit Bonfire Era.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 20x Bronze Ingot
* 20x Hardwood Strip
* 20x Hardwood Plank
* 20x Thin Rope
