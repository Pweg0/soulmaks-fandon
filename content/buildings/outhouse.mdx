**Outhouse** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A makeshift outhouse for collecting feces. Learned from Furniture (Technology) in Medium Building (Technology) - Tier 2, Bonfire Era.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 10x Hardwood Plank
* 5x Ceramic Mold
* 40x Thatch
