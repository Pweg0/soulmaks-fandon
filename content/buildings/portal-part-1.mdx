# Portal Part 1

**Portal Part 1** is an item in *Soulmask*.

## Acquisition
All three Portal Parts can be found in various Dungeons and Holy Ruins sites. They are always found together.

## Usage
It can be combined in the Mysterious Stone Table along with Portal Part 2 and Portal Part 3 to craft a functional Portal.
