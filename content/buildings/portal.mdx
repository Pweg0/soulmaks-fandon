# Portal

<br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>







**Portal** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
It Pode ser fabricado by combining Portal Part 1, Portal Part 2, and Portal Part 3 in the Mysterious Stone Table.

## Uso
It can be placed anywhere on the map as long as you can build in that area, but the Data Update 3 Mask Node needs be repaired in the Plateau Pyramid in order to use it. 

Placing 2 portals allows you to teleport between them, consuming 100 Mask Energy. 

### Notas
*There is a limit to how many active portals Tribes can have.
*By default you can only teleport with currently worn armor and the items on your hotbar. Trying to teleport while carrying additional items will produce the following message: *You cannot carry any item in your package*. (Travel through portals with items can be permitted in settings.)
*You can't teleport with deployed Tribesmen/mounts. (This can also be allowed in settings.)
