# Rainwater Collector

}}
**Rainwater Collector** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

It holds 10,000 units of Water that replenishes when it rains.

Pressing E next to it with a Water Bottle, Water Tank, or Water Bucket in the inventory will fill that container.

It can also be used directly to refill o jogador's hydration level.
