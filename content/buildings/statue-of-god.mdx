**Statue of God** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
During a PVE NPC invasion, tribesmen gather around the statue, those who are not involved in patrols.

### Buffs and Benefits
The Statue of God can provide various buffs to players and their Tribesmen. These buffs enhance different aspects of gameplay, such as increased health regeneration, improved combat abilities, and faster crafting times. The exact nature of the buffs may vary based on the specific type of statue and its level.

### Worship and Interaction
Players and Tribesmen can interact with the statue to activate its buffs. This interaction often involves a ritual or offering, which can be a gameplay mechanic encouraging players to explore and gather particular items.

The statue has 60 (6x10) inventory slots for storing items of any type.

### Strategic Placement
The placement of the Statue of God is strategic. It should be placed in a central location within the player's base to maximize its protective and beneficial radius. This ensures that the buffs provided by the statue cover the most critical areas of the base.

### Construction
Constructing the Statue of God is a significant investment. Players need to collect a variety of building materials, which can be time-consuming and resource-intensive. The construction process can also be a communal effort, involving multiple players or Tribesmen.

### Upgrades
The statue can be upgraded over time. Each upgrade level increases the strength and duration of the buffs provided. Upgrading the statue requires additional resources and possibly the completion of specific in-game quests or challenges.

### Defense Role
Beyond providing buffs, the Statue of God can also play a role in the defense of the base. Its presence may deter certain types of enemies or reduce the frequency of invasions by hostile forces.  Notably, non combatant tribesmen will retreat to any nearby Statue of God at any sign of hostility - place them in well defended areas to keep the enemy from capturing or killing them.
