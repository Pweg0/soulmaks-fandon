It is a variant form of the Stone Wall building piece, and can be accessed two ways:
*During placement of a Stone Wall, hold Z and choose the  form from the list, or 
*Hold E over a previous tier Thatch Door Frame or Wooden Door Frame piece with a Stone Wall in the inventory, and directly upgrading-in-place to a .

## Notas
*When retrieved,  is placed in the inventory as a separate item than Stone Wall. It does not stack with any of the other Stone Wall variants. 

*While in  form it will not count as an option for directly upgrading previous tier Window Walls or Double Door Frames. During placement however, those other variants can still be accessed to change it using the Z key.

*Sometimes Tribesmen AI has difficulty navigating single door frame pieces, and bunch up together causing a bottleneck. If this happens, change the piece to a Double Door Frame to allow them more room to move.
