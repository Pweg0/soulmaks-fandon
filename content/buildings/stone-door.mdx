# Stone Door

}}
**Stone Door** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Acquisition
It is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Bronze Pit Bonfire: Advanced Building.
## Variants
 has one variant, which can be accessed by holding Z:
:60px Stone Double Door
## Usage
Stone Door and Stone Double Door can only be placed within the respective Door Frame and Double Door Frame variants of the Thatch Wall, Wooden Wall, Stone Wall, or Blackstone Wall building pieces.

Once in place, it can be open and closed. The Double Door variant opens both doors at once.
