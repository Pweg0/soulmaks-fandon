# Stone Fence

}}
**Stone Fence** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Bronze Pit Bonfire: Advanced Building.
## Variantes
 has two other variants, which can be accessed by holding Z:
:60px Stone Stair Guardrail
:60px Stone Half-height Stair Guardrail
Unlike the default  which can be placed at the edge of any foundation or flooring, guardrail pieces can only be placed on the sides of stairs or half-height stairs.
