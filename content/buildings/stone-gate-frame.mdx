**Stone Gate Frame** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A Stone Gateframe for building your base. Learned with Stone Door (Technology) in Advanced Building (Technology) - tier 3, Bronze Pit Bonfire

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 60x Stone Brick
* 40x Hardwood Plank or optional Premium Hardwood Plank
