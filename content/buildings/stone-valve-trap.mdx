**Stone Valve Trap** is a building in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A valve trap made of Stone for a safe base. Learned from Guard Facility (Technology) in Advanced Building (Technology) - tier 3, Bronze Pit Bonfire

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Building Workshop
* 5x Hardwood Plank
* 5x Bronze Ingot or Iron Ingot
