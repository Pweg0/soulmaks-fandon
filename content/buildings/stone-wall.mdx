# Stone Wall

}}
**Stone Wall** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Bronze Pit Bonfire: Advanced Building.
## Variantes
 has a few variants, which can be accessed by holding Z and choosing the form to use:
:60px Stone Window Wall
:60px Stone Door Frame
:60px Stone Double Door Frame
:60px Stone Half Wall
:60px Stone Sloping Wall
:60px Stone Half-height Sloping Wall
:60px Stone Inverted Sloping Wall
:60px Stone Inverted Half-height Sloping Wall
### Notas
*Any tier of Door, Double Door, or Window piece can be placed in the respective Stone Door Frame, Stone Double Door Frame, or Stone Window Wall to become an openable door or window.
*Retrieving Stone  Door Frame and Stone Double Door Frame will place that item in the inventory in that form. They can be reverted to any of the other forms during placement.
*Retrieving any of Stone Wall's other variants will return a plain Stone Wall to the inventory.
