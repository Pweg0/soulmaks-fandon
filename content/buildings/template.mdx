# Template

}}
## Descrição
An essential heating device constructed at the Building Workshop with iron ingot, premium leather, hardwood plank, leather rope, and rubber. It provides heating in a large area and is learned with Temperature Rise Technology, tier 2 of the Iron Pit Bonfire.
## Obtenção
Can be constructed through the following:
*Building Workshop                     
*50x Iron Ingot
*40x Premium Leather
*20x Hardwood Plank
*20x Leather Rope
*20x Rubber
