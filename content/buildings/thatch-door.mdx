# Thatch Door

\}\}
**Thatch Door** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
It is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Campfire: Basic Building.
## Variantes
 has one variant, which can be accessed by holding Z:
:60px Thatch Double Door
## Uso
Thatch Door and Thatch Double Door can only be placed within the respective Door Frame and Double Door Frame variants of the Thatch Wall, Wooden Wall, Stone Wall, or Blackstone Wall building pieces.

Once in place, it can be open and closed. The Double Door variant opens both doors at once.
