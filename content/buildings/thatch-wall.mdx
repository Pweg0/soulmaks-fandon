# Thatch Wall

<br/>






}}
**Thatch Wall** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Acquisition
It is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Campfire: Basic Building. 
## Variants
 has a few variants, which can be accessed by holding Z and choosing the form to use:
:60px Thatch Window Wall
:60px Thatch Door Frame
:60px Thatch Double Door Frame
:60px Thatch Sloping Wall
:60px Thatch Inverted Sloping Wall
### Notes
*Any type of Window piece can be placed in the Thatch Window Wall to become an openable window.
*Retrieving Thatch Door Frame and Thatch Double Door Frame will place that item in the inventory in that form. They can be reverted to any of the other forms during placement.
*Retrieving any other variant of Thatch Wall will return a plain Thatch Wall to the inventory.
*The flavor text for the item says to press T to switch between the variants, but the key is actually Z.
