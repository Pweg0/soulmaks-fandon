# Wooden Door

}}
**Wooden Door** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Medium Building.
## Variantes
 has one variant, which can be accessed by holding Z:
:60px Wooden Double Door
## Uso
Wooden Door and Wooden Double Door can only be placed within the respective Door Frame and Double Door Frame variants of the Thatch Wall, Wooden Wall, Stone Wall, or Blackstone Wall building pieces.

Once in place, it can be open and closed. The Double Door variant opens both doors at once.
