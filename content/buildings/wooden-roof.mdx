# Wooden Roof

}}
**Wooden Roof** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
 is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Medium Building.
## Variantes
 has a few variants, which can be accessed by holding Z and choosing the form to use:
:60px Wooden Pitched Roof
:60px Wooden Roof Corner
:60px Wooden Recessed Roof
:60px Wooden Gable Roof
:60px Wooden Inverted Gable Roof
## Notas
*The flavor text for the item says to press T to switch between the variants, but the key is actually Z.
