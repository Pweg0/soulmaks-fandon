# Wooden Wall

}}
**Wooden Wall** is a building item in *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
It is unlocked via the Knowledge and Technology tree under Bonfire: Medium Building. 
## Variantes
 has a few variants, which can be accessed by holding Z and choosing the form to use:
:60px Wooden Window Wall
:60px Wooden Door Frame
:60px Wooden Double Door Frame
:60px Wooden Right-sloping Wall
:60px Wooden Left-sloping Wall
### Notas
*Any type of Window piece can be placed in the Wooden Window Wall to become an openable window.
*Retrieving Wooden Door Frame and Wooden Double Door Frame will place that item in the inventory in that form. They can be reverted to any of the other forms during placement.
*Retrieving any other variant of Wooden Wall will return a plain Wooden Wall to the inventory.
*The flavor text for the item says to press T to switch between the variants, but the key is actually Z.
