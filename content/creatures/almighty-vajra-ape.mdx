## Overview
 is the first variant of the Vajra Ape. It can be summoned in front of the Table Mountain Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Vajra Ape Mark and a Vajra Ape Key. If the player has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Almighty Vajra Ape Head can also be harvested from it.
