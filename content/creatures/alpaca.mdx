**Alpaca** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

They Pode ser encontrado em some Ruínas Antigas in the Rainforest or near the Pit Hill area (Requer trap)

## Descrição
They have a slender body and neck. The head is small with large and pointed ears. Docile and intelligent, they have human-like characteristics. Besides wild species, there are domesticated species that are widely used as draft animals and suitable for breeding. When displeased, they will expel secretions from their nose or spit at others to vent their anger.
