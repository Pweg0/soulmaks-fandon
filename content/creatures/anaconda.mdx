**Anaconda** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Its true might lies not in its venomous fangs, but in its unparalleled strength. It tightly wraps its body around its prey, gradually exerting pressure until it pulverizes them, then consumes the prey entirely.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
## Tactics
When engaging with an Anaconda it is best to kill them quickly or keep your distance if possible using spears, bows, or throwing weapons, because their coil/squeeze attack can be completely debilitating and outright kill lower level jogadores.
