**Armadillo Lizard** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Adorned with armor-like scales, Esta criatura can spew venomous blood from its eyes. When threatened, it swiftly curls into a ball, tucking its tail into its mouth, and rolls away to safety.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
