# Armadillo

\}\}
**Armadillo** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
A protective armor covers the sides of its torso and the outside of its four limbs. The armor consists of bony plates and scales, divided into front and rear sections by movable transverse bands. These bands are connected by elastic skin, allowing the body to curl up into a ball as a defense mechanism against predators. It carries viruses and transmits diseases.
