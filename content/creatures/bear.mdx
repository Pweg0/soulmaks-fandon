# Bear

**Bear** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Its enormous physique instills fear in other creatures, allowing it to occasionally steal from other predators instead of catching its own prey. Its massive molars can crush animal bones, and its long limbs allow it to move quickly for long periods. In times of food scarcity, it also temporarily becomes a scavenger.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
