**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Overview
 is the second variant of the Mammoth Guardian. It can be summoned in front of the Alpine Land Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Mammoth Mark and a Mammoth Key. If the player has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion,  Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Mammoth Statue
*Mammoth Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Mammoth Leather Armor
**Mammoth Leather Pants
**Mammoth Leather Boots
**Mammoth Gloves
**Mammoth Necklace
**Mammoth Ring
