## Visão Geral
 is the second variant of the Saber-toothed Predator. It can be summoned in front of the Rainforest Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar.
## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Saber-toothed Predator Mark and a Saber-toothed Predator Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Berserk Saber-toothed Predator Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Saber-toothed Predator Statue
*Saber-toothed Predator Set, which includes the following pieces:

## Fight
The boss has high mobility and fast attacks that apply bleed stacks. It will attack and roar almost immediately after being summoned, and its tactics involve running around the arena and jumping at jogadores to attack them before quickly retreating. Do not ever keep your back turned to this boss, as it has very fast attacks and moves around the arena a lot.
### Recommendations
It is recommended to have at least Iron Weapons and Iron Gear before attempting this boss. Spear, or Gauntlets are ideal for fast damage, however, it is doable with any weapon. Dual-blades and Poisons are not recommended for this fight as it applies a status effect which will immediately trigger a roar rendering you incapacitated. Firm Will III - [Flint Tribe Exclusive], Steel Body III, and/or Hardened Body are good green perks to have if you choose to use status effects. Refined Bandages and Blood Activating Cream or Hematogenesis Cream are important to regenerate lost health and remove bleeding stacks.
### Melee
This tactic will give you the quickest and highest damage in a short amount of time, as the fight will drag on if you keep your distance.
*Start the fight with a long rage weapon spear or bow to avoid the initial roar. Keep your distance initially, as the roar has a large AOE.
*This boss has two phases. A slow stalk and a sprint.
*The major difference between this variant and the Fierce Saber-toothed Predator, in either of these two phases the boss may pounce twice. Be aware of this if you are at considerable distance from the boss. Its eyes will not glow red like the first variant before it pounces in the sprint phase. As stated before any status effect applied to the boss will trigger a roar. The bleed damage is also significantly greater.
*Slow stalk phase: Walk towards the boss to force it to jump back and then pounce. To avoid the pounce dodge forward slightly to the left or right depending on which side the boss jumps back from. The goal is to roll underneath the boss while it is mid-pounce, so you end up behind it.
*Sprint phase: Walk towards it and follow the direction it sprints in.  Since you no longer have the red glow as a cue, when the boss is perpendicular to you it will pounce. After the pounce the boss will strike once or three times in front of it, which should allow you to turn around and hit it from behind, getting in one to two good hits.
You do not need to sprint at all during the fight, except to get back to the boss after a pounce. Otherwise you will waste stamina needed for dodge rolls and attacks. If you get hit, apply a bandage only immediately after successfully dodging a pounce, as this is the only decent window of time to do so.

If your melee damage is not high enough, try to keep a distance, dodge roll whenever possible, and use ranged attacks.
