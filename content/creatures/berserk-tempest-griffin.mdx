**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Overview
 is the second variant of the Tempest Griffin. It can be summoned in front of the Northern Wasteland Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Tempest Griffin Mark and a Tempest Griffin Key. If the player has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Berserk Tempest Griffin Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
*Berserk Tempest Griffin Specimen
*Tempest Griffin Statue
* Tempest Griffin Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Tempest Griffin Feather Armor
**Tempest Griffin Feather Pants
**Tempest Griffin Feather Boots
**Tempest Griffin Gloves
**Tempest Griffin Necklace
**Tempest Griffin Ring
