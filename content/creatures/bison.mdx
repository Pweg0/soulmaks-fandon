# Bison

**Bison** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
They have a massive head and front of body, with shoulders resembling camel humps. The neck is short and thick. Both male and female bisons have short, curved horns, which can be Usado para combat and defense.

There exists an Elite version in some areas. Can be tamed by capturing a young calf from Certain Elites. Use Fruits like Papaya and Guava to lure them in a Large Trap.
