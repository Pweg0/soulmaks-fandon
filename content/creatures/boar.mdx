# Boar

**Boar** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Wild boars are robust in size with rough fur, and a head shaped like a real plow, capable of upturning a large amount of soil and boulders. These creatures are extremely aggressive and will not hesitate to attack when threatened, so hunters must be extremely cautious when pursuing them.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
