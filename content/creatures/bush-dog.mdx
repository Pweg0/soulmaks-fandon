# Bush Dog

\}\}**Bush Dog** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
They always act collectively and have highly social relationships within the community. During the hunting process, they have specific roles. The experienced leader is always the main attacker, while the other members are responsible for chasing and harassing the prey.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
