**Capybara** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
They have a broad head, short, round ears, and small eyes. Their limbs are short and stout, with four toes on the forelegs and three toes on the hind legs, with slight webbing between the toes. They possess a gentle temperament, prefer tranquility, and move slowly. When threatened, they quickly jump into the water to escape. Easily domesticated and bred.

## Basic information
* Role: Livestock
* Growth time: fast
* Capture: Medium Trap
* Pen: Capybara Pen
* Fodder: Nut, Peanut, Cashew, Quinoa, Pumpkin, Corn, Banana, Pineapple, Potato, Tomato, Papaya, Guava, Capybara Feed, Refined Capybara Feed
