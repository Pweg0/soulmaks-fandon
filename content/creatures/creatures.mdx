# Creatures

**Creatures** that can be encountered, battled and entered into the Hunting log in *Soulmask*.

## List of creatures
### Domesticated
Some of the above creatures can be further captured and domesticated for particular roles.  More details may be found on the Farming page.


## Elite
Elite animals are unique in that they dynamically level and harvest for higher quality ingredients and loot. 
Their spawns also cannot be blocked by buildings, unlike non-elite animals. 

Elite versions of animals drop rarer items (fangs, claws, etc) for use in higher-tier armor and weapons. After the player reaches a certain Hunting proficiency level, the larger beasts can also be harvested for their heads, which can be crafted into specimens as base decorations.
