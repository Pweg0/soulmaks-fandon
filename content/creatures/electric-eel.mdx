# Electric Eel

}}
**Electric Eel** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Their body is slender and cylindrical, the head is flat, and the body surface is smooth and scaleless. It has a unique organ that releases powerful electric waves to shock its prey. It is an extremely dangerous inhabitant of freshwater rivers!
