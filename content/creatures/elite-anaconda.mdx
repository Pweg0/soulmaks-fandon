# Elite Anaconda

<br/>|image=Elite Anaconda.jpg}}
**Elite Anaconda** é uma criatura em *Soulmask*.

It is a larger and more aggressive version of the regular Anaconda, and Pode ser encontrado em Beast Lairs and other high-danger areas in Wetland, Creek, and Jungle regions.

* Level: 35
* Notable Loot: Elite Anaconda Carcass Chunk, Anaconda Skin
*Harvestable Loot: Premium Fresh Meat
*Stats are dynamic by their level
