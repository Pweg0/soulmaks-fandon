# Elite Bear

**Elite Bear** é uma criatura em *Soulmask*.

This more fearsome variant of the Bear Pode ser encontrado em some Beast Lairs across the world of Soulmask.
* Level: 35+
* Notable Loot: Rare Claw, Rare Thick Hide
* Harvestable Loot: Rare Fresh Meat, Rare Beast Bone, Rare Beast Blood, Rare Carcass Chunk
* Stats are dynamic by their level
