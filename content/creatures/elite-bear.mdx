# Elite Bear

<br/><br/><br/><br><br/><br/>

**Elite Bear** is a creature in *Soulmask*.

This more fearsome variant of the Bear can be found in some Beast Lairs across the world of Soulmask.
* Level: 35+
* Notable Loot: Rare Claw, Rare Thick Hide
* Harvestable Loot: Rare Fresh Meat, Rare Beast Bone, Rare Beast Blood, Rare Carcass Chunk
* Stats are dynamic by their level
