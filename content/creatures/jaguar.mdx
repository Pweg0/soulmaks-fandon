# Jaguar

**Jaguar** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
They possess well-developed muscles and a streamlined body shape, which grants them extremely powerful running and jumping abilities. Solitary by nature, they are top predators skilled at surprise attacks! It is believed a lucky and powerful warrior can tame one to serve as his mount!

There exists an Elite version in some areas, which cannot be captured.

## Basic information
* Role: Mount
* Growth Time: slow
* Capture: Medium Trap
* Saddle: Panther Saddle
* Normal Speed: fast
* Dash Speed: very fast
* Swim Speed: fast
* Carrying Capacity: 250
* Fodder: Fresh Meat, Premium Fresh Meat, Rare Fresh Meat, Fish
