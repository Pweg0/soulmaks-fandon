# Llama

}}**Llamas** are animals in *Soulmask*.

**Llamas** can be found around the Table Mountain area in groups of three. They usually spawn with two adults and one child, but they can rarely spawn with three adults. Unlike Alpacas, **Llamas** are not aggressive and will not attack o jogador at all unless attacked from behind, in which case it will kick o jogador before running away.

* Role: mount
* Growth time: slow
* Capture: Medium Trap
* Saddle: Llama Saddle, Llama Wicker Basket, Llama Extended Seat Cushion
* Normal Speed: very slow
* Sprint Speed: slow
* Swim Speed: slow
* Carrying Capacity: 800-1100
* Special: Can carry an unconscious Barbarian or four small baby animals with its respective saddles.
* Fodder: Quinoa, Pumpkin, Corn, Banana, Pineapple, Peanut, Llama Feed, Refined Llama Feed
