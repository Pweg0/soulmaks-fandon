## Visão Geral
 is the third variant of the Mammoth. It can be summoned in front of the Alpine Land Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Mammoth Mark and a Mammoth Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion,  Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Golden Mammoth Statue
*Advanced Mammoth Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Advanced Mammoth Leather Armor
**Advanced Mammoth Leather Pants
**Advanced Mammoth Leather Boots
**Advanced Mammoth Gloves
**Advanced Mammoth Necklace
**Advanced Mammoth Ring
## How To Kill
The Mammoth is considered by most to be the most difficult, near impossible bosses to defeat...especially in solo/offline mode.  In actual fact, this can often be attributed to an incorrect strategy or by not investing time in the preparation to give the best chance of surviving the fight.  The Mammoth is 100% not a close quarters fight, so going toe-to-toe hoping to tank it will likely result in failure.  Instead, look at medium to long range methods of fighting.  If you are not geared up for this, invest in that first.  There is a useful "setup guide" here that will take you through the process of setting up a hunter/bowman specifically to take on the Mammoth: https://youtu.be/qkk5SY0KG9E
