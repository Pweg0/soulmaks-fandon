**Monkey** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
It has two long white beards and is used to staying still at one spot to eat and rest. Mainly eats fruits, nuts, and other plant foods, and likes to steal food from others. Can be domesticated.

## Basic information
Domesticated Monkeys will gather fruits and nuts from the nearby environment and deposit them in a Monkey Storage Nest.  They can also ride on a player's shoulder and help collect nearby loot.

* Role: Pet
* Structure: Monkey Storage Nest
* Collects: Wild Fruit, Pineapple, Nut, Mushroom, Banana
