# Mutant Rat

**Mutant Rat**  is an animal in *Soulmask*.

A rat that has undergone bodily mutations after being corroded by something over time.

There is an elite version that can be found in certain areas.
## Location
s are commonly found in "Ancient Labs", two of which can be found in the northwest Giant Crater region close to the entrance of the Holy Ruins in the area. They also populate the front courtyard of the same Holy Ruins alongside Scorpions.
## Notes
*The use of 'mutant' plus the idea of an ancient laboratory is a reference to the utilization of rats in modern science experiments.
