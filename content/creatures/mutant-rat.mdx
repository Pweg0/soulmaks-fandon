# Mutant Rat

**Mutant Rat**  is an animal in *Soulmask*.

A rat that has undergone bodily mutations after being corroded by something over time.

There is an elite version that Pode ser encontrado em certain areas.
## Localização
s are commonly Encontrado em "Ancient Labs", two of which Pode ser encontrado em the northwest Giant Crater region close to the entrance of the Ruínas Sagradas in the area. They also populate the front courtyard of the same Ruínas Sagradas alongside Scorpions.
## Notas
*The use of 'mutant' plus the idea of an ancient laboratory is a reference to the utilization of rats in modern science experiments.
