# Ostrich

\}\}**Ostrich** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
As it walks, it strides confidently, exuding an impressive presence. When it runs, it moves with the swiftness of a darting arrow. Though its wings are muscular, flight is beyond its grasp. Yet, there are tales of it gliding down from great heights. And watch out for its mighty legs; their stomping power is fearsome.

## Basic information
* Role: Mount
* Growth time: slow
* Normal Speed: medium
* Dash Speed: very fast
* Swim Speed: medium
* Carrying Capacity: 300
* Special: Gliding ability reduces fall damage
* Fodder: Wild Fruit, Quinoa, Pumpkin, Corn, Banana, Pineapple, Peanut,  Ostrich Feed, Refined Ostrich Feed
