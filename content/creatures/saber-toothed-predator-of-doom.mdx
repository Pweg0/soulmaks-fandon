**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.
## Overview
 is the third variant of the Saber-toothed Predator. It can be summoned in front of the Rainforest Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar.
## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Saber-toothed Predator Mark and a Saber-toothed Predator Key. If the player has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Saber-toothed Predator of Doom Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Golden Saber-toothed Predator Statue
*Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Leather Armor
**Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Leather Pants
**Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Leather Boots
**Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Gloves
**Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Necklace
**Advanced Saber-toothed Predator Ring
