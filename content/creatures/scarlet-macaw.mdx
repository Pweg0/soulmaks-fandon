# Scarlet Macaw

<br/>





}}**Scarlet Macaw** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Their bodies are vibrant in color, and their beaks are sickle-shaped and extremely sharp. Immune to poison, likes fruits and flowers. Relatively easy to be trained by humans and can mimic human speech through domestication.  Macaws can ride on a Tribesman's shoulder.

## Basic information
* Role: Pet
* Capturing: Small Trap
* Pen: Parrot Rack
* Fodder: Nut, Locust, Poisonous Bug, Peanut, Cashew, Corn Flour, Quinoa, Parrot Feed, Refined Parrot Feed
