# Tapir

**Tapir** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Tapirs have small eyes, oval ears, upright and not very agile. They have a pig-like body shape and a retractable short snout. They have a body with a rounded back and tapers at the front - ideal for moving quickly through dense undergrowth.
