**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Visão Geral
 is the third variant of the Tempest Griffin. It can be summoned in front of the Northern Wasteland Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Tempest Griffin Mark and a Tempest Griffin Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion, Tempest Griffin of Doom Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
*Tempest Griffin of Doom Specimen
*Golden Tempest Griffin Statue
*Advanced Tempest Griffin Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Advanced Tempest Griffin Feather Armor
**Advanced Tempest Griffin Feather Pants
**Advanced Tempest Griffin Feather Boots
**Advanced Tempest Griffin Gloves
**Advanced Tempest Griffin Necklace
**Advanced Tempest Griffin Ring
