# Tortoise

\}\}**Tortoise** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
They have a large head, long neck, and sturdy, column-like limbs. On their backs is a massive, hard carapace with a prominent central ridge. They move slowly and have a long lifespan. They have a gentle temperament and retract their limbs, head, and tail into their carapace for defense when threatened.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
