# Turkey

}}
**Turkey** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
It has distinctive features, with a tall and muscular physique, exposed head and neck, a fleshy protuberance above the nostrils, and flexible, retractable skin lumps on the top of its head. It feeds on berries, nuts, seeds, and invertebrates such as insects. Easily domesticated and bred.

## Basic information
*Role: Livestock
*Growth Time: fast
*Capturing: Small Trap
*Pen: Turkey Coop
*Fodder: Nut, Locust, Poisonous Bug, Peanut, Cashew, Corn Flour, Quinoa, Turkey Feed, Refined Turkey Feed
*Drops: Turkey Meat, Fresh Meat, Feathers
