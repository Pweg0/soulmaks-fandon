**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

## Visão Geral
 is the third variant of the Vajra Ape. It can be summoned in front of the Table Mountain Pyramid by crafting its token at the central altar. 

## Rewards
On defeat,  drops a Vajra Ape Mark and a Vajra Ape Key. If o jogador has achieved the "Top Hunter" Hunting promotion,  Head can also be harvested from it.

Defeating it for the first time unlocks the the following in the Knowledge and Technology tree:
* Specimen
*Golden Vajra Ape Statue
*Advanced Giant Ape Set, which includes the following pieces:
**Advanced Giant Ape Leather Armor
**Advanced Giant Ape Leather Pants
**Advanced Giant Ape Leather Boots
**Advanced Giant Ape Gloves
**Advanced Giant Ape Necklace
**Advanced Giant Ape Ring
There is some good strategy tips for beating the hardest ape (Vajra Ape of Doom) even in solo here: https://youtu.be/RzlqiHdRBDM.  It's a long 45 minute fight but proves it is do-able even by yourself.
