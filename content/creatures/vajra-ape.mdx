# Vajra Ape

**** is a Temple Guardian in *Soulmask*.

It can be summoned at the Plateau Pyramid in the Table Mountain region.<br>
400px

There are three variants, each increasing in difficulty:
*Almighty Vajra Ape
*Berserk Vajra Ape
*Vajra Ape of Doom
Vajra Ape is a tough boss to beat boasting a high HP and strong attacks.  There are tips that can be used to evade some of his attacks, like sticking close to a tree and moving round it so there is always a gap between Ape and Player.  Long range weapons can also be useful for this fight, as he tends to go off into the distance to collect projectiles.  He will also climb the tree to regenerate his health, this can be disrupted though with an arrow or spear to the head where he will fall to the ground.  When this happens he will be dazed and a healthy amount of damage can be applied to his head.  A useful video demonstrating this technique can be seen on Youtube here: https://youtu.be/-HOld46Eg6I
