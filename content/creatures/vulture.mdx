# Vulture

}}
**Vulture** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
As the largest raptors, vultures can easily snatch prey larger than themselves. Adults have partially exposed heads and necks. They can easily reach into a carcass's abdominal cavity with their bare heads, and their hooked beaks can effortlessly pierce and tear through tough hide, to remove those heavy internal organs.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
