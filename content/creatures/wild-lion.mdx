# Wild Lion

}}**Wild Lion** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Its body is well-proportioned, the limbs are medium-long, and it has strong running ability. Their canines and carnassials are extremely developed. It moves stealthily, lurking in dark places for long periods of time, waiting for opportunities to sneak up on unknowing prey and deliver a surprise attack that will kill them with one blow.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
