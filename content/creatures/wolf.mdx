# Wolf

\}\}**Wolf** is an animal in *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
As typical carnivorous animals, dominant male wolves always target the weak or elderly when organizing and leading hunts. At first, they encircle their prey from various directions, slowly closing in. When the timing is right, they launch a sudden attack. If the prey tries to escape, they pursue it relentlessly. To conserve energy, they often divide into multiple teams, taking turns until they successfully capture it.

There exists an Elite version in some areas.
