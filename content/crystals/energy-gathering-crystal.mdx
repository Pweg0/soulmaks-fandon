**Energy-gathering Crystal** é um item em *Soulmask*.

It is the final tier of Pedra Solar.

## Obtenção
It Pode ser fabricado in the Mysterious Stone Table out of Pedra Solars, with three separate blueprints utilizing each type:
*100 Pedra Solar (Not enough energy)
or
*50 Pedra Solar (Lost energy)
or
*20 Pedra Solar (Enough energy)

## Uso
The  is a very high energy storage item, holding a maximum of 10,000 Energy. It is unique compared to regular Pedra Solars in that it is not consumed on use, but the used energy is subtracted from the total. It does not stack however, making it more space-effective to craft as needed.
