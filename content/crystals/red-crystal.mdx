## Usage
Red Crystals can be dragged into the 6 diamond-shaped slots surrounding a mask in a Mysterious Stone Table.

They are not consumed on use, and may be swapped out at will for others as long as they do not go over the Crystal Limit of the current mask's level.

They may be uncrafted in a Mysterious Stone Table to get Yellow Crystals.
### Crystal Limit
Crystal Limit refers to how many slots each crystal takes up within a mask. Each Red Crystal has its own Cyrystal Limit stat, usually ranging somewhere from 15 to 40 depending on the crystal level and type. 

For example, a Mask at level 2 may only have the capacity to hold 50 crystals, so you might be able to have two 25-slot red crystals OR a single 37.5-slot crystal. At higher mask levels, this maximum slot number increases to anywhere from 200 to 350 at level 10, depending on the mask.
### Crystal Tiers
Like armor and weapons, Red Crystals can range from White to Red in power/rarity.
