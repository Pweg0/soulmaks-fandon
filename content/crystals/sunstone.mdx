# Pedra Solar

**Pedra Solars** are a set of items in *Soulmask*.

They are used to replenish mask energy.

There are four tiers, each holding more energy than the previous:
*Pedra Solar (Not enough energy) holds 100 Energy
*Pedra Solar (Lost energy) holds 200 Energy
*Pedra Solar (Enough energy) holds 500 Energy
*Energy-gathering Crystal, crafted from other Pedra Solars, holds 10,000 Energy
