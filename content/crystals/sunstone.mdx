# Sunstone

**Sunstones** are a set of items in *Soulmask*.

They are used to replenish mask energy.

There are four tiers, each holding more energy than the previous:
*Sunstone (Not enough energy) holds 100 Energy
*Sunstone (Lost energy) holds 200 Energy
*Sunstone (Enough energy) holds 500 Energy
*Energy-gathering Crystal, crafted from other Sunstones, holds 10,000 Energy
