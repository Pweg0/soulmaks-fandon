# Banana

**Banana** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A nutritious food item that can be obtained from barracks, monkeys, and monkey storage nests, used in various crafting recipes.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Barracks
* Monkey
* Monkey Storage Nest

## Usage
Banana's are used in various crafting recipes, including:
*Bee-drawing Syrup
*Yeast
*Fruit Wine
*Fruit Kebab
*Juice
