# Cashew

**Cashew** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A versatile ingredient obtained from cashew seeds and mid-tier scout chests, used in various crafting recipes.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Cashew Seed
*Mid-Tier Scout chests

Cashew's are used in various crafting recipes, including:
*Cooking Oil
*Plant Essence
*Cashew Seed
*Refined Turkey Feed
*Refined Parrot Feed
