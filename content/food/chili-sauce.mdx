**Chili Sauce** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A spicy sauce made using a cooking stove with chili and crude salt, used in various crafting recipes including Sauced Meat, Chili Sauce Locusts, and Spicy Soup.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Cooking Stove: (Chili x2 + Crude Salt x1)
