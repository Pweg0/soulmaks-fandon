# Cooking Oil

}}
**Cooking Oil** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A vital cooking ingredient made using an oil press with nuts, peanuts, cashews, and their seeds, used in various crafting recipes including Premium Steak, Fried Rare Steak, Smoked Ham, Fried Potato Bun, Fried Taco, Fried Pumpkin Ring, Poison Proof Mod 1, Refined Capybara Feed, Grease Tank, Sticky Trap, Iron Ballista Projectile, and Aseptic.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Oil Press: (Nut x1 + Peanut x1 + Cashew x1 + Peanut Seed x1 + Cashew Seed x5)
