# Corn

**Corn** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A staple food obtained from the barracks, used in various crafting recipes including Corn Flour, Roasted Corn, Corn Seed, Blood Crystal, Alpaca Feed, Ostrich Feed, and Llama Feed.
