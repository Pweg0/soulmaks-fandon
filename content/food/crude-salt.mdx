**Crude Salt** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
A basic seasoning made using a cooking stove with salt mine, Usado em various crafting recipes including Refined Salt, Chili Sauce, Glass, Sauced Meat, and Premium Jerky.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido by mining piles of salt in Salt Pans (a type of Pit), or through crafting at a Cooking Stove (Salt Mine x1).
