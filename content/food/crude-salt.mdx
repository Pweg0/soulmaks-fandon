# Crude Salt

}}
**Crude Salt** is a food in *Soulmask*.

## Description
A basic seasoning made using a cooking stove with salt mine, used in various crafting recipes including Refined Salt, Chili Sauce, Glass, Sauced Meat, and Premium Jerky.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained by mining piles of salt in Salt Pans (a type of Pit), or through crafting at a Cooking Stove (Salt Mine x1).
