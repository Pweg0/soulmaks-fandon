# Fresh Meat

\}\}
**Fresh Meat** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Fresh Meat is a crucial ingredient for crafting various recipes, but it will decay and spoil within 2.5 hours. Fortunately, there are several methods available to prevent food from decaying. 

## Obtenção
Fresh Meat can be harvested from specific animals such as the following:

* Cocunut Crab
* Turkey
* Capybara
* Guava Tree
* Tapir
* Iracoterio
* Alpaca
* Llama
* Boar

## Uso
Fresh Meat's Usado em various crafting recipes, including:

* Carrion
* Cooked Meat
* Broth
* Fried Potato Bun
* Fruit Kebab
