**Fried Rare Steak** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A gourmet dish made using a worktop with cooking oil, refined salt, and optionally rare fresh meat or rare cooked meat. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Berserk Vajra Ape Token and Vajra Ape of Doom Token.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:

* Worktop: (Required: Cooking Oil x8 + Refined Salt x5) (Optional: Rare Fresh Meat x1 + Rare Cooked Meat x2)
