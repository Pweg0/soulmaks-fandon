# Fried Rare Steak

<br/>







}}
**Fried Rare Steak** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A gourmet dish made using a worktop with cooking oil, refined salt, and optionally rare fresh meat or rare cooked meat. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Berserk Vajra Ape Token and Vajra Ape of Doom Token.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:

* Worktop: (Required: Cooking Oil x8 + Refined Salt x5) (Optional: Rare Fresh Meat x1 + Rare Cooked Meat x2)
