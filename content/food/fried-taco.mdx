# Fried Taco

}}
**Fried Taco** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A tasty snack made using a worktop with cooking oil, tortilla, cooked meat, and boiled egg. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Titan Mammoth Token, Berserk Mammoth Token, and Mammoth of Doom Token.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Worktop: (Cooking Oil x5 + Tortilla x2 + Cooked Meat x5 + Boiled Egg x2)
