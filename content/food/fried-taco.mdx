**Fried Taco** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A tasty snack made using a worktop with cooking oil, tortilla, cooked meat, and boiled egg. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Titan Mammoth Token, Berserk Mammoth Token, and Mammoth of Doom Token.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Worktop: (Cooking Oil x5 + Tortilla x2 + Cooked Meat x5 + Boiled Egg x2)
