# Frozen Potato

}}
**Frozen Potato** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A preserved ingredient made using a drying rack with a potato. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Cooked Potato, Fried Potato Bun, Blood Crystal, Turkey Feed, and Capybara Feed.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
