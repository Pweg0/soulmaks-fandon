**Fruit Kebab** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A delightful skewer made using a cooking stove with fresh meat, banana, and pineapple. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Almighty Vajra Ape Token.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:*Cooking Stove: (Fresh Meat x5 + Banana x2 + Pineapple x2)**
