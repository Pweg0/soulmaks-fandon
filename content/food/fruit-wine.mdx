\}\}
**Fruit Wine** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A refreshing beverage made using a brewing stand with tree bark, yeast, and a cup. Optional ingredients include wild fruit, banana, pineapple, and papaya. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Almighty Vajra Ape Token.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Brewing Stand: (Required: Tree Bark x5 + Yeast x2 + Cup x1) (Optional: Wild Fruit x5 + Banana x5 + Pineapple x5 + Papaya x5)
