# Guava

**Guava** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
Guava is a tropical fruit in Soulmask, Obtido de scout boxes and supply chests. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Acidic Fluid, Bee-drawing Syrup, Antidote, Guava Seed, and Juice.
