# Guava

**Guava** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Guava is a tropical fruit in Soulmask, obtained from scout boxes and supply chests. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Acidic Fluid, Bee-drawing Syrup, Antidote, Guava Seed, and Juice.
