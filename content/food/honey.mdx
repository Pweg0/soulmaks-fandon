# Honey

**Honey** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
Honey is a sweet and versatile ingredient in Soulmask, harvested from beehives. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Mead, Coagulation Cream, Hematogenesis Cream, Fried Pumpkin Ring, Mixed Jam, Iced Cocoa, and Blood Crystal.
