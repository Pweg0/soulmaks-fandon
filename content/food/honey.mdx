# Honey

**Honey** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Honey is a sweet and versatile ingredient in Soulmask, harvested from beehives. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Mead, Coagulation Cream, Hematogenesis Cream, Fried Pumpkin Ring, Mixed Jam, Iced Cocoa, and Blood Crystal.
