**Mixed Jam** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A delightful mixture made using a worktop with salsa, papaya, and honey, used in various crafting recipes including the Vajra Ape of Doom Token and Berserk Vajra Ape Token.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:*Worktop: (Salsa x4 + Papaya x2 + Honey x4)
