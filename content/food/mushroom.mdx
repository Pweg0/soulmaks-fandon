# Mushroom

**Mushroom** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A versatile ingredient found on the ground (mostly in the rainforest) and obtained from monkeys, used in various crafting recipes.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Ground (Mostly in the Rainforest)
*Monkey

*

## Usage
Mushroom's are used in various crafting recipes, including:
*Mushroom Soup
*Antidote
*Roasted Mushroom
*Psychedelic Pot
