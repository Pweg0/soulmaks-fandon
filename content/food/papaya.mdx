# Papaya

**Papaya** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A tropical fruit Obtido de the barracks, Usado em various crafting recipes including Bee-drawing Syrup, Yeast, Fruit Wine, Plant Essence, Mixed Jam, Papaya Seed, Refined Alpaca Feed, Refined Capybara Feed, and Refined Llama Feed.
