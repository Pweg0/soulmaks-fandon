# Papaya

**Papaya** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A tropical fruit obtained from the barracks, used in various crafting recipes including Bee-drawing Syrup, Yeast, Fruit Wine, Plant Essence, Mixed Jam, Papaya Seed, Refined Alpaca Feed, Refined Capybara Feed, and Refined Llama Feed.
