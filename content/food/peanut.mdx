# Peanut

**Peanut** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A nutritious food obtained from the barracks, used in various crafting recipes including Stasis Dissipation Soup, Cooking Oil, Peanut Seed, Refined Turkey Feed, Refined Parrot Feed, Refined Ostrich Feed, and Refined Llama Feed.
