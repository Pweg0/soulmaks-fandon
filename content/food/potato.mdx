# Potato

**Potato** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A nutritious ingredient obtained by planting seeds or from barracks, used in various crafting recipes.
## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Planted as a Seed
*Barrack

*

*

*
## Usage
Potato's are used in various crafting recipes, including:
*Roasted Potato
*Cooked Potato
*Frozen Potato
*Blood Crystal
*Turkey Feed
*Capybara Feed
