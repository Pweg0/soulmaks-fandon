# Potato

**Potato** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A nutritious ingredient obtained by planting seeds or from barracks, Usado em various crafting recipes.
## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Planted as a Seed
*Barrack

*

*

*
## Uso
Potato's are Usado em various crafting recipes, including:
*Roasted Potato
*Cooked Potato
*Frozen Potato
*Blood Crystal
*Turkey Feed
*Capybara Feed

*

*
## Notas
Fertilizer usage: **Compost**

Can be planted directly into Farmland. No seed needed.
