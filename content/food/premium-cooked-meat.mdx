# Premium Cooked Meat

}}
**Premium Cooked Meat** is a food in *Soulmask*. It provides a buff when consumed.
## Description
A high-quality dish made using a campfire with premium fresh meat, used in various crafting recipes including Sauced Meat, Premium Steak, and Fish Hotchpotch.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Campfire: (Premium Fresh Meat x1)
