# Premium Fresh Meat

}}
**** is a food item in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A top-tier meat obtained through the butcher's table from various premium carcass chunks.

## Acquisition
Can be retrieved from large creature corpses using a butcher's knife.

Can be crafted at a Butcher's Table from one of the following: Elite Alligator Carcass Chunk, Premium Carcass Chunk, or Premium Thick Hide Carcass Chunk.

## Usage
 is used in various crafting recipes, including:

*Premium Cooked Meat
*Premium Steak
*Premium Jerky
*Fish Hotchpotch

It is also required as a component in summoning Berserk Saber-toothed Predator and Sky-shading Tempest Griffin.
