# Premium Jerky

}}
**Premium Jerky** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A preserved meat made using a drying rack with crude salt and either premium fresh meat or rare fresh meat, used in various crafting recipes including Smoked Ham.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Drying Rack: (Required: Crude Salt x4) + (Either: Premium Fresh Meat x1 ***OR*** Rare Fresh Meat x2)
