**Premium Jerky** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A preserved meat made using a drying rack with crude salt and either premium fresh meat or rare fresh meat, Usado em various crafting recipes including Smoked Ham.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Drying Rack: (Required: Crude Salt x4) + (Either: Premium Fresh Meat x1 ***OR*** Rare Fresh Meat x2)
