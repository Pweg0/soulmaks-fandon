# Premium Steak

<br/>







}}
**Premium Steak** é uma comida em *Soulmask*. It provides a buff Quando consumido.
## Descrição
A high-quality dish made using a cooking stove with refined salt, cooking oil, and either premium fresh meat or premium cooked meat, Usado em various crafting recipes including Almighty Vajra Ape Token.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Cooking Stove: (Required: Refined Salt x2 + Cooking Oil x2) + (Either: Premium Fresh Meat x1 ***OR*** Premium Cooked Meat x2)
