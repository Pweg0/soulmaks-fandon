# Psychedelic Pot

}}
**Psychedelic Pot** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A mystical concoction made using a worktop with mushrooms, cocoa powder, and a bowl, used in various crafting recipes including Berserk Mammoth Token and Mammoth of Doom Token.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Work Top: (Mushroom x5 + Cocoa Powder x5 + Bowl x1)
