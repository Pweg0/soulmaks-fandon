# Pumpkin

**Pumpkin** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A versatile food item obtained from scout camps and barracks, used in various crafting recipes.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Scout Camps
*Barracks

*Roasted Pumpkin
*Pumpkin Salad
*Fried Pumpkin Ring
*Pumpkin Seed
*Refined Alpaca Feed
*Refined Capybara Feed
