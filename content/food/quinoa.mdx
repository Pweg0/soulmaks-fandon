# Quinoa

**Quinoa** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A versatile grain obtained from barracks or fortress, used in various crafting recipes including Quinoa Porridge, Seafood Quinoa Rice, Blood Crystal, and Parrot Feed.
