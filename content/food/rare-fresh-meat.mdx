## Acquisition
Dropped by Temple Guardians and Elite versions of creatures, or crafted at a Butcher's Table from Rare Thick Hide Carcass Chunk, Rare Carcass Chunk, or Elite Anaconda Carcass Chunk.

## Usage
Crafting Table Blueprints:
*Fried Rare Steak (Worktop)
*Premium Jerky (Drying Rack)
It is also required as a component to summon the Berserk Saber-toothed Predator, Saber-toothed Predator of Doom, Berserk Tempest Griffin, and Tempest Griffin of Doom.
