# Refined Salt

<br/>






}}
**Refined Salt** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A purified seasoning made using a cooking stove with crude salt and lime. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Premium Steak, Fried Rare Steak, Smoked Ham, Spicy Soup, Fish Hotchpotch, Seafood Quinoa Rice, Refined Alpaca Feed, Refined Capybara Feed, Refined Ostrich Feed, and Refined Llama Feed.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Cooking Stove: (Crude Salt x2 + Lime x1)
