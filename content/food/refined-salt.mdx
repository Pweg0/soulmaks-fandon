# Refined Salt

}}
**Refined Salt** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A purified seasoning made using a cooking stove with crude salt and lime. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Premium Steak, Fried Rare Steak, Smoked Ham, Spicy Soup, Fish Hotchpotch, Seafood Quinoa Rice, Refined Alpaca Feed, Refined Capybara Feed, Refined Ostrich Feed, and Refined Llama Feed.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Cooking Stove: (Crude Salt x2 + Lime x1)
