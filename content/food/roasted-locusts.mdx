# Roasted Locusts

}}
**Roasted Locusts** is a food in *Soulmask*. It provides a buff when consumed.
## Description
Roasted Locusts are a unique food item in Soulmask, made by roasting locusts over a campfire. They are used in crafting recipes such as Chili Sauce Locusts.
*
## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following: 

* Campfire: (Locust x2)
## Usage
Roasted Locusts are used in various crafting recipes, including:
*Chili Sauce Locusts
