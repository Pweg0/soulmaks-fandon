# Roasted Potato

}}**Roasted Potato** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Roasted Potato is a simple yet satisfying food item in Soulmask, made by roasting a combination of fresh and frozen potatoes over a campfire. While it currently has no specific crafting uses, it serves as a basic nourishment option.
