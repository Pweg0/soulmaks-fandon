# Roasted Pumpkin

}}**Roasted Pumpkin** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Roasted Pumpkin is a savory food item in Soulmask, made by roasting a pumpkin over a campfire. It is a key ingredient in crafting recipes such as the Titan Mammoth Token.
