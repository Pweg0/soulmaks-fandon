# Salsa

<br/>






}}**Salsa** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Salsa is a zesty condiment in Soulmask, made from chili and tomato. It enhances various dishes and is used in multiple crafting recipes, such as Pumpkin Salad, Mixed Jam, and Mysterious Tisane.
