# Sauced Meat

<br/>







}}
**Sauced Meat** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Sauced Meat is a flavorful dish in Soulmask, made using chili sauce, crude salt, and various types of cooked meat. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Spicy Soup.
*
## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Cooking Stove: (Required: Chili Sauce x2 + Crude Salt x4) (Optional: Cooked Meat x1 + Premium Cooked Meat x1 + Rare Cooked Meat x1)
**

Sauced Meat is used in various crafting recipes, including:
