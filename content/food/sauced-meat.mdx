# Sauced Meat

}}
**Sauced Meat** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
Sauced Meat is a flavorful dish in Soulmask, made using chili sauce, crude salt, and various types of cooked meat. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Spicy Soup.
*
## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following:
*Cooking Stove: (Required: Chili Sauce x2 + Crude Salt x4) (Optional: Cooked Meat x1 + Premium Cooked Meat x1 + Rare Cooked Meat x1)
