# Seafood Quinoa Rice

<br/><br/><br/>






}}**Seafood Quinoa Rice** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Seafood Quinoa Rice is a hearty dish crafted using quinoa, fish, refined salt, and a bowl. This nutritious meal is essential in crafting powerful items such as Worship Sky-shading Tempest Griffin, Berserk Tempest Griffin Token, and Tempest Griffin of Doom Token in Soulmask.
*
## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Worktop: (Quinoa x8 + Fish x5 + Refined Salt x4 + Bowl x1)*

*

*

Seafood Quinoa Rice is used in various crafting recipes, including:*Worship Sky-shading Tempest Griffin.
