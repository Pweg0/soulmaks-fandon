# Tomato

**Tomato** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Tomato is a versatile food item in Soulmask, obtained from tomato seeds. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Salsa, Acidic Fluid, Tomato Seed, and Red Paint.
