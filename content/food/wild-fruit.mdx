# Wild Fruit

**Wild Fruit** é uma comida em *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
Wild Fruit is a versatile ingredient in Soulmask, Obtido de monkeys, monkey storage nests, and harvesting bushes. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Yeast, Fruit Wine, Juice, and Monkey Storage Nest.
