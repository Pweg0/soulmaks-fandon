# Wild Fruit

**Wild Fruit** is a food in *Soulmask*.
## Description
Wild Fruit is a versatile ingredient in Soulmask, obtained from monkeys, monkey storage nests, and harvesting bushes. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Yeast, Fruit Wine, Juice, and Monkey Storage Nest.
*
## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following:
*Monkey
*Monkey Storage Nest
*Harvesting Bushes
