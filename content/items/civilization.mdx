# Civilization

**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

The Civilization mask can be chosen as a starter mask, or acquired later from level 25 Ruínas Antigas.  

Its upgrades focus on stamina and defense for improved survivability.
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Healer Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Ironblood Guard. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, high-frequency attacks trigger emergency rescue. The higher the frequency, the more HP is restored.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to continually recover 5 HP por segundo. It will fuel HP this way until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to stop it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
