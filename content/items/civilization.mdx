# Civilization

**** is a mask in *Soulmask*.

The Civilization mask can be chosen as a starter mask, or acquired later from level 25 Ancient Ruins.  

Its upgrades focus on stamina and defense for improved survivability.
## Mimicry Mode
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Healer Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Ironblood Guard. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Yellow Crystals.
### Passive Effect
When in Mimicry Mode, high-frequency attacks trigger emergency rescue. The higher the frequency, the more HP is restored.
### Active Effect (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to continually recover 5 HP per second. It will fuel HP this way until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to stop it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Sunstones can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
