# Conquest

**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

The Conquest mask can be chosen as a starter mask, or acquired later from level 25 Ruínas Antigas.  

Its upgrades focus on stealth and combat.
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Slayer Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, each attack grants a 1.5% Lifesteal effect.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to give the wearer a 20% chance to lower a target's Resilience when hitting one of their weak spots. It will keep up this effect until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to stop it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
