# Earth Worship Container

**Earth Worship Container** is an item in *Soulmask*.

Earth Worship Containers appear as loot in the Rift Valley Holy Ruins.  They are used to summon the Saber-Toothed Predator of Doom.

You can see the location and how to find here: https://youtu.be/t4q7x1eGIks
