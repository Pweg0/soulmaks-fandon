# Explorer

}}
**** is a mask in *Soulmask*.

Its upgrades focus on resistance to negative status effects and harsh environments.
## Acquisition
The Explorer mask may be acquired from the level 60 Holy Ruins in the northwest Frost Canyon region.
## Mimicry Mode
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Special Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module (Explorer). 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Yellow Crystals.
### Passive Effect
When in Mimicry Mode, resistances to radiation, poison, and temperature are raised considerably.
### Active Effect (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to activate a shield that lasts for 8 seconds. The shield blocks all energy/radiation-powered attacks, and resists attacks from small low-speed weapons.
