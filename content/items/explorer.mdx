# Explorer

\}\}
**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

Its upgrades focus on resistance to negative status effects and harsh environments.
## Obtenção
The Explorer mask may be acquired from the level 60 Ruínas Sagradas in the northwest Frost Canyon region.
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Special Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module (Explorer). 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, resistances to radiation, poison, and temperature are raised considerably.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to activate a shield that lasts por 8 segundo(s). The shield blocks all energy/radiation-powered attacks, and resists attacks from small low-speed weapons.
