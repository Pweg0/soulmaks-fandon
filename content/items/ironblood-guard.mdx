# Ironblood Guard

<br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>






}}
**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

It can be assembled in the Mysterious Stone Table by repairing the Ironblood Guard (Broken) item Encontrado em Ancient Dungeons.

Its upgrades focus on damage reduction and stamina.
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Vigilance Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Ironblood Guard. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, 20% of the damage taken in melee combat is reflected.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to continually recover stamina. It will fuel stamina this way until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to stop it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
