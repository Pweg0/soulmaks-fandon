# Shadow Walker

\}\}
**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

Its upgrades focus on stealth and damage.
## Obtenção
The Shadow Walker mask may be acquired from the level 55 Ruínas Sagradas in the Scorching Volcano region. (Entrance is on the southwest side of the volcano.) 
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Shadow Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module (Shadow Walker). 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, releasing Backstab has a chance to launch a Deadly Blow.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to become invisible to any creature (it is less effective the closer the wearer is to the target). It will keep the wearer hidden until the mask's energy is depleted, V is pressed again to stop it, an attack is launched by the wearer, or they are discovered by the target. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect until the wearer attacks or is discovered, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
