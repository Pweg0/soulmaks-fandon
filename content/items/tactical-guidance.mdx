# Tactical Guidance

**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

Its upgrades focus on improving the performance of deployed Tribesmen.
## Obtenção
The Tactical Guidance mask may be acquired from the level 50 Ruínas Sagradas in the Giant Crater region.  
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Protection Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module (Tactical Guidance). 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, a deployed tribesman who is dying will only take 50% damage and not be the target of any attacks.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to grant the deployed tribesman Superarmor. It will keep up the armor until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to stop it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
