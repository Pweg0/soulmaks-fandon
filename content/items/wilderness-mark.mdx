# Wilderness Mark

**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

Its upgrades focus on archery and hunting.

## Obtenção
The Wilderness Mark mask Pode ser saqueado de the Ruínas Sagradas to the northwest of the Jungle Cave region. Poison Proof gear is necessary to traverse the dungeon and surrounding areas.
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Hunting Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Wilderness Mark. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, Muscle Strength and Endurance are increased, and the Firing Speed of Bows is increased by 30%.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Note: A bow must be equipped for this effect to become available.

Pressing V while in 's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to turn all arrows fired into homing arrows that track their target. It will affect arrows this way until the mask's energy is depleted, V is pressed again to stop it, or another weapon is equipped. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
