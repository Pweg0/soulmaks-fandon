# Ruínas Antigas Masmorra

**Ruínas Antigas Dungeons** are locations in *Soulmask*.

## frameless Ruínas Antigas Masmorra right|frameless
Ruínas Antigas Dungeons are dangerous zones guarded by well equipped Plunderers, fierce creatures, and robotic inimigos called Mechanical Cores and Slayers.  They often contain advanced loot including Cristal Vermelhos, Mask components, and exotic materials. 

There are four  sites, located in different areas of the map. (For reference regarding names of regions, see Locations.) 

For a more in depth list of NPC loot, see the inimigo and Chest Loot Table.__TOC__

## River Valley 【T3】16~25
**Location:** Northeast of the Table Mountain Pyramid <br>
**How to get there:** Access is from a cave toward the bottom of the cliff facing the river.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 20-25<br>
**inimigos:** Plunderer, Plunderer Elite, Mechanical Core, Slayer, Slayer S, Slayer X<br>
**Primary dungeon loot:**
*Holy Relic Key - River Valley
*Portal Part 1
*Portal Part 2
*Portal Part 3
*Mysterious Stone Table
*Mysterious Stone Table Part 1
**Notable chest loot:**
*Weapon & Gear Durability Mods, Cristal Vermelhos, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar
**Tablets:**
Two Ancient Tablets can be found inside the dungeon area. One Ancient Tablet ("The Passage") in the cave before the dungeon itself, and one Divine Tablet ("The Gift") in a puzzle-locked room.



## Wetland 【T5】26~35
**Location:** West of the Table Mountain Pyramid <br>
**How to get there:** Access is from a cave toward the bottom of the cliff facing the river.<br>
**Precautions to take:** The entire entrance cave and the majority of the ruins themselves are covered in a poisonous fog. Poison Proof gear is mandatory.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 31-35<br>
**inimigos:** Centipede, Arrow-Poison Frog, Elite Arrow-Poison Frog, Mechanical Core, Mechanical Core D, Mechanical Core-Malfunction, Slayer, Slayer S, Slayer X<br>
**Primary dungeon loot:**
*Holy Relic Key - Wetland
*Restrictive Module (Torch of Eternity)
*Portal Part 1
*Portal Part 2
*Portal Part 3
*Mysterious Stone Table
*Mysterious Stone Table Part 1
**Notable chest loot:**
*Damage and Poison Proof Mods, Antidote, Cristal Vermelhos, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar, Bloodstone
**Tablets:**
One Ancient Tablet ("Servants") can be found inside the cave before the dungeon itself.


## Northern Wasteland 【T8】41~50
**Location:** Northeast of the Northern Wasteland Pyramid <br>
**How to get there:** The dungeon entrance is visible from the ground, but there is a ruined maze-like area before getting to it.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 41-45<br>
**inimigos:** Plunderer, Plunderer Elite, Mechanical Core, Slayer, Slayer S, Slayer X<br>
**Primary dungeon loot:**
*Holy Relic Key - Northern Wasteland
*Restrictive Module (Wilderness Mark)
*Portal Part 1
*Portal Part 2
*Portal Part 3
*Mysterious Stone Table
*Mysterious Stone Table Part 1
**Notable chest loot:**
*Radiation and Heat Proof Mods, Adhesive, Chalcedony, Excitant, Cristal Vermelhos, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar
**Tablets:**
One Ancient Tablet ("Desecration") Pode ser encontrado em a large building in the courtyard area before the actual dungeon entrance.


## Dark Forest 【T11】51~60
**Location:** Eastern part of the Dark Forest snowy region, deep in an icy crater<br>
**How to get there:** Access is from the south side of the crater (guarded by a Snow Leopard), which leads to a partially frozen lake. The cave entrance is on the far north side, so some platforming across small ice floes and/or swimming might be necessary.<br>
**Precautions to take:** Chill Proof *and* Poison Proof gear is highly recommended, one for the lake and ice jets, and the other for the venomous spider guards.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 51-60<br>
**inimigos:** Spider, Elite Spider, Mechanical Core, Slayer, Slayer S, Slayer X<br>
**Primary dungeon loot:**
*Holy Relic Key - Dark Forest
*Restrictive Module (Shadow Walker)
*Portal Part 1
*Portal Part 2
*Portal Part 3
*Mysterious Stone Table
*Mysterious Stone Table Part 1
**Notable chest loot:**
*Damage and Chill Proof Mods, Mysterious Leather, Amber, Chalcedony, Cristal Vermelhos, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar, various animal parts
