# Ancient Tablet

**Ancient Tablets** are points of interest in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Ancient Tablets and their larger variant, Divine Tablets, are leftover fragments of knowledge from a lost civilization. Collecting them gives some insight as to how people lived and worshipped at the time.

## Locations
Ancient and Divine Tablets can be found throughout the world of *Soulmask*. They are most often found on the walls of Mysterious Ruins, inside Ancient Dungeons and Holy Ruins, or at the base of Temple Guardian Pyramids.

## Usage
Deciphering the inscriptions on these tablets rewards the player with Knowledge Points, which can be used to unlock blueprints on the Knowledge and Technology tree.
