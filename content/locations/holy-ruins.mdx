Ruínas Sagradas are dangerous dungeons guarded by well equipped Plunderers, fierce creatures, and mechanical inimigos. They contain advanced loot including new masks, red crystal mask mods, and exotic materials not found anywhere else.

There are five Ruínas Sagradas sites, located in different areas of the map. (For reference regarding names of regions, see Locations.) 

For a more in depth list of NPC loot, see the inimigo and Chest Loot Table.__TOC__
## Obstacles
Ruínas Sagradas contain a variety of mechanical inimigos, traps, puzzles, obstacles, and hidden rooms. The most common include:
### Inimigos
inimigo types in the area surrounding the ruins will vary depending on the biome. They usually include one or more elite versions. 

Within the ruins are usually the following:
*Slayer and Slayer S robots
*Mechanical Core robots, which come in designations of I, II, X, E, and Y, each with slightly different tactics
### Traps
*Arrow trap: Triggered by a pressure plate in the floor, usually placed right before a corner leading to a room. Can be avoided by jumping over the plate.
*Spike trap: Appears as black plates on the floor with white holes. Triggered when stepped on. Can be avoided by jumping over.
*Giant blades: Huge blades swinging from the ceiling in a predictable rhythm. Easily avoided with the right timing.
*Flame, poison, or ice trap: Large barrels at the end of corridors that shoot a stream of fire, poison, or ice fog into the hallway at timed intervals. Cannot be destroyed or turned off.
### Puzzles
*Target shoot: Mechanisms within bow/spear distance must be shot at to be activated. Usually all visible from view of the door they open. If one doesn't activate when shot at or hit with a spear, try moving closer to the target, since there's a limited range it works from.
*Target activate: Mechanisms that look the same as the targets, but must be manually activated. Usually scattered across a range of nearby rooms, within a maze, or behind a hidden door.
*Brazier puzzle: All braziers in the area must be lit up. Typically follows a simplified "Lights Out" solution pattern, where each brazier affects the two adjacent ones.
*Cylinder puzzle: These appear like vertical cylinder locks. The goal is to rotate the sections to align with the pedestal base.
### Hidden Rooms
Occasionally there will be a visible crack in one of the dungeon walls. You can use the mask scan to see the cracks more clearly, since they're lit up briefly like other hallway details. Hitting the wall with a hammer a couple of times will break it, revealing a room or corridor behind. Usually contains a chest or hidden mechanism to open a door nearby.

## Rift Valley Ruínas Sagradas【T3】16~25
**Location:** Northwest of the Rainforest Pyramid <br>
**How to get there:** Access is behind the waterfall, SW of the Ruínas Sagradas icon on the map. The entrance is through a winding cavern and then a ruined maze, heavily guarded by Plunderers.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 26-30<br>
**inimigos:** Plunderer, Plunderer Elite, Mechanical Core I, Mechanical Core II, Mechanical Core S (Eite), Mechanical Core X (Leader), Slayer, Slayer S (Elite)<br>
**Primary chest loot:**
*Torch of Eternity Mask
*Earth Worship Container
**Notable chest loot:**
*Mysterious Alloy, Mineral Oil, Bloodstone, Meteorite Ore, Gold Ingot, Silver Ingot, Cristal Amarelo, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar

## Jungle Cave Ruínas Sagradas【T5】26~35
**Location:** Northwest Jungle Cave region, straight west of the Lakeside Forest Barbarian Fortress<br>
**How to get there:** Access is through a winding tunnel with two entrances, one west of the northmost lake, and one NE of the next lake over.<br>
**Precautions to take:** The entire surrounding region, entrance cave, and ruins themselves are covered in a poisonous fog. Poison Proof gear is mandatory.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 36-40<br>
**inimigos:** Centipede, Scorpion, Elite Scorpion, Mechanical Core, Elite Mechanical Core S, Mechanical Core X (Leader), Slayer, Elite Slayer S<br>
**Notable traps:** Arrow traps, poison jets<br>
**Primary chest loot:**
*Wilderness Mark Mask
*Water Worship Container
**Notable chest loot:**
*Mysterious Alloy, Jade, Bloodstone, Meteorite Ore, Crystal, Chalcedony, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar<br>
Note: This area is one of the only places in the game to find Jade.

**NPC/inimigo Level:** 46-50<br>
**inimigos:** Scorpion, Mutant Rat, Elite Mutant Rat, Mechanical Core, Elite Mechanical Core S, Mechanical Core X (Leader), Slayer, Elite Slayer S<br>

Note: This area is one of the only places in the game to find Mysterious Magnets.

## Scorching Volcano Ruínas Sagradas【T10】46~55
**Location:** Inside the volcano<br>
**How to get there:** Start from the Iron Ore Mine on the west side of the volcano and follow the path S/SE. Access is through a cavern that leads to the inside of the volcano. Follow the pathways around the edges of the lava to reach the Ruínas Sagradas entrance.<br>
**NPC/inimigo Level:** 46-55<br>
**inimigos:** Armadillo Lizard, Elite Armadillo Lizard, Mechanical Core, Elite Mechanical Core S, Mechanical Core Y (Leader), Slayer, Elite Slayer S<br>
**Notable traps:** Arrow traps, fire jets<br>
**Primary chest loot:**
*Shadow Walker Mask
*Fire Worship Container
*Ancient Text of Civilization
**Notable chest loot:**
*Mysterious Alloy, Mysterious Fiber, Gold Obsidian, Chalcedony, Bloodstone, Cristal Verde, Pedra Solar
Note: This area is one of the only places in the game to find Mysterious Fiber.
