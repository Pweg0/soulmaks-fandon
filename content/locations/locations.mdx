# Locations

The world of Soulmask is large, and there are many locations to find. Exploration is rewarded, as there are often hidden areas or secrets to find tucked away.
## Points of Interest
An interactive World Map is available for finding the following specific points of interest:

* 32px Beast Lair: Small areas or caves guarded by an elite beast.
* 32px32px32px Pit: Small, medium, or large-sized caves or sunken areas containing a concentration of ore nodes. Sometimes labeled on the map as Mines. Usually guarded by people or beasts, in larger numbers than Ruínas Antigas. Often contains an elite version of the guard at the bottom.
* 32px Barbarian Camp: A scouting camp manned by 3-5 Barbarian Outcasts or individuals from one of the 3 hostile tribes, and sometimes an additional animal guard (Wolf or Boar). Usually includes a Scout who can be deterred to reveal the map in the area or add a new "?" location to explore.
*32px Barbarian Barrack: A ground-level, large camp set up like a small town populated by one of the 3 Tribes. Main challenges include a Tribe Chief and an Elite at the center.
*32px Barbarian Fortress: A maze-like ancient stone fort populated by Barbarians, Outcasts, or one of the 3 Tribes plus an elite version.
*32px Ruínas Antigas: The remains of structures from an ancient civilization, typically guarded by 3 regular beasts, 1 elite beast, or a group of Outcasts. Usually contains an Ancient Relic chest, as well as a supplies chest if guarded by Outcasts. An Ancient Tablet can sometimes be found on the walls as well.
*32px Ruínas Antigas Large: A collection of ruined structures like the smaller Ruínas Antigas, but spread out with more inimigos.
*32px Masmorra: A portal-accessed instanced and timed Masmorra. The portal usually Requer keys and/or crystals to activate.
*32px Ruínas Antigas Masmorra: A large dungeon with loot, occasional puzzles or hidden doors, and many Mechanical Core and Slayer type inimigos. Can sometimes contain portal pieces or masks, but not guaranteed.
*32px Ruínas Sagradas: Very large dungeons, often containing masks, portal pieces, Mysterious Stone Table pieces, and rare crafting materials. Contains the same inimigos as Ruínas Antigas Dungeons.
*32px Mysterious Ruins: A columned-structure guarded by Outcasts, containing a pedestal that can be activated to repair a mask node.
*32px Pyramid: The location of one of the world bosses and a pedestal to repair one of the mask's Data Nodes.
*32px Mysterious Portal: One of the permanent world portal structures.
*frameless|32pxframeless|32px Ancient Tablet: A stone tablet or stele that can be read to reveal a quote and unlock 1-2 tech points. There are two types: regular Ancient Tablets, and larger Divine Tablets.

## Map Regions
The map of *Soulmask* can be divided into regions according to their prominent biomes. Sometimes a point of interest will be referred to with the surrounding biome as a qualifier, like "Jungle Holy Ruin", "Desert Pyramid", or "River Barracks". This map is what those names refer to.
