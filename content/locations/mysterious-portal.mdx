# Mysterious Portal

**Mysterious Portals** are locations in *Soulmask*.__NOTOC__
## frameless Mysterious Portal right|frameless
Mysterious Portals allow teleportation between different regions. They are unlocked and operated with Cristal Verdes.

A player will not be able to activate or use one until the Vajra Ape Temple Guardian is defeated and their Mask's Data Update 3 node is repaired inside the Pyramid.
## Localizações
There are 16 portals spread across the map, represented by different types of icons in the portal selection screen. Six representing regions, one for each Pyramid, and one for each Ruínas Sagradas:

### Regions
Six portals connect to map regions:

### Pyramids
File:Mysterious Portal Pyramid Icon.png Each of the five Pyramids has a portal nearby, for easier access to Temple Guardians:
*Rainforest Pyramid (Saber-toothed Predator)
*Hill Pyramid (Fogfrog)
*Plateau Pyramid (Vajra Ape)
*Desert Pyramid (Tempest Griffin)
*Snow Mountain Pyramid (Mammoth)
### Ruínas Sagradas
File:Mysterious Portal Ruínas Sagradas Icon.png Each of the five Ruínas Sagradas has a portal nearby:
*Rift Valley Ruínas Sagradas
*Jungle Cave Ruínas Sagradas
*Giant Crater Ruínas Sagradas
*Scorching Volcano Ruínas Sagradas
*Frost Canyon Ruínas Sagradas
## Notas
In general  are not actively guarded, but there may be a stray Wolf, Wasteland Wolf, or Bush Dog pack that spawned nearby.
