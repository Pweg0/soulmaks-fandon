# Pyramid

**Pyramids** are locations in *Soulmask*.
## frameless Pyramids right|frameless
Five Ancient Pyramids loom over the landscape of *Soulmask*, each guarded by a fierce Temple Guardian. Every guardian has three tiers, each increasing in level and difficulty. To summon a guardian, you must bring the appropriate offerings to their altar. 

Defeating a guardian allows access to their Pyramid, where mysterious technology awaits to unlock new mask abilities. The guardians themselves are also good sources of rare materials and Cristal Azuls.
## Rainforest Pyramid
