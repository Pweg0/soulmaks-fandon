# Nature Enlightenment

}}
**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*.

It can be assembled in the Mysterious Stone Table by repairing the Nature Enlightenment (Broken) item that drops in Ancient Dungeons. 

Its upgrades focus on mastery over beasts.
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Resonance Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Nature Enlightenment. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, small and medium-sized non-elite beasts will not attack o jogador. 

This includes predatory animals that typically strike first, like Bush Dogs, Jaguars, Wolves (wolves will howl as usual, but not give chase), Arrow-Poison Frogs, and Centipedes. Prey animals will still run, but have a much smaller radius of awareness. Animals higher than o jogador's level will not be affected.

For this reason it is a very good mask to take when fighting the Fogfrog Temple Guardian, because the smaller frogs in the arena are no longer a threat.

Taking a follower with you will somewhat negate the effect, as the animals will be alerted to them.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to summon a nearby beast to fight for you as a familiar. The beast will remain by your side until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to release it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Pedra Solars can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
