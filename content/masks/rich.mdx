# Rich

**** is a mask in *Soulmask*.

The Rich mask can be chosen as a starter mask, or acquired later from level 25 Ancient Ruins.  

Its upgrades focus on improving perception and archery.
## Mimicry Mode
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Sniping Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Yellow Crystals.
### Passive Effect
When in Mimicry Mode, damage dealt by Bows is increased by 15%.
### Active Effect (V)
Pressing V while in  's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to raise the wearer's chance to hit a target's weak spot when attacking, and increases Crit by 5%. It will keep up the effect until the mask's energy is depleted or V is pressed again to stop it. 

Replenishing the mask's energy with Sunstones can prolong the effect, but it must be continually maintained because the energy drain is very rapid.
