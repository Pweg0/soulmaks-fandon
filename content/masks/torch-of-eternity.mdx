# Torch of Eternity

**** é uma máscara em *Soulmask*. 

Its upgrades focus on keeping the wearer alive after injury.
## Obtenção
The Torch of Eternity mask can be acquired from the level 30 Ruínas Sagradas in the Northern Rainforest region, northwest of the Rainforest Pyramid. 
## Modo Mimetismo
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Holy Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Torch of Eternity. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Cristal Amarelos.
### Efeito Passivo
When in Mimicry Mode, 25% of damage taken is converted into healing.
### Efeito Ativo (V)
Pressing V while in 's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to trigger immunity to all external damage por 20 segundo(s), but also makes the wearer immobile for the duration. There is a 60-second cooldown period after the immunity ends.
