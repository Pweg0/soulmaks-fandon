# Torch of Eternity

**** is a mask in *Soulmask*. 

Its upgrades focus on keeping the wearer alive after injury.
## Acquisition
The Torch of Eternity mask can be acquired from the level 30 Holy Ruins in the Northern Rainforest region, northwest of the Rainforest Pyramid. 
## Mimicry Mode
When level 10 is reached, the mask's final node ("Holy Mode") can be unlocked in the Mask Node Repair screen using Restrictive Module - Torch of Eternity. 

To activate the mimicry, press C. It costs 5 Yellow Crystals.
### Passive Effect
When in Mimicry Mode, 25% of damage taken is converted into healing.
### Active Effect (V)
Pressing V while in 's Mimicry Mode will use the mask's energy to trigger immunity to all external damage for 20 seconds, but also makes the wearer immobile for the duration. There is a 60-second cooldown period after the immunity ends.
