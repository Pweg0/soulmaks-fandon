# Blood Activating Cream

\}\}
**Blood Activating Cream** é um medicamento em *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
It Pode ser fabricado in the Lv.1 Brewing Stand upgraded by adding Brewing Stand Part 1. Pode ser encontrado em chests and drop from humanoid inimigos in various locations. 

## Uso
Can be used to recover (*10%*Max HP + 150*) HP after a short animation. Has a 30s cooldown.

Used to summon the Poison Fogfrog.

## Notas
*All medicines of this type (such as Coagulation Cream and Hematogenesis Cream) share the same cooldown, meaning that using it will not allow you to use other Cream items until the cooldown passes.
