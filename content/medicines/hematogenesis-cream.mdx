# Hematogenesis Cream

<br/><br/><br/><br/>






}}
**Hematogenesis Cream** é um medicamento em *Soulmask*.

## Obtenção
It Pode ser fabricado in the Lv.3 Brewing Stand upgraded by adding Brewing Stand Part 1, Brewing Stand Part 2 and Brewing Stand Part 3. Pode ser encontrado em chests and drop from humanoid inimigos in various locations. 

## Uso
Can be used to recover (*40%*Max HP + 600*) HP and 100 Stamina after a short animation. Has a 30s cooldown.

Used to summon the Fogfrog of Doom.

## Notas
*All medicines of this type (such as Blood Activating Cream and Coagulation Cream) share the same cooldown, meaning that using it will not allow you to use other Cream items until the cooldown passes.
