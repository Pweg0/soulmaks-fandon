# Purified Poison

}}
**Purified Poison** é um item em *Soulmask*.
## Obtenção
Pode ser fabricado manually or at a Brewing Stand from Venom and either Carrion or Poisonous Mushroom.

## Uso
 can be dragged onto arrows in the inventory to coat them, similar to using Item Mods. Consumes the poison on use, 1x per arrow.
