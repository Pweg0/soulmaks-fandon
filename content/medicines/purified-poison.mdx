# Purified Poison

}}
**Purified Poison** is an item in *Soulmask*.
## Acquisition
Can be crafted manually or at a Brewing Stand from Venom and either Carrion or Poisonous Mushroom.

## Usage
 can be dragged onto arrows in the inventory to coat them, similar to using Item Mods. Consumes the poison on use, 1x per arrow.
