# Venom

}}
**Venom** is a medicine and animal drop in *Soulmask*.
## Descrição
A potent substance Obtido de centipedes, scorpions, spiders, and arrow-poison frogs, Usado em various crafting recipes.

## Obtenção
Pode ser obtido through the following animals:

* Centipede
* Scorpion
* Spider
* Arrow-Poison Frog

*

*
## Uso
Venom is Usado em various crafting recipes, including:

* Locust Pesticide
* Herbicide
* Aseptic
* Purified Poison
