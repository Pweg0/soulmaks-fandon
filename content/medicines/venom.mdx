# Venom

}}
**Venom** is a medicine and animal drop in *Soulmask*.
## Description
A potent substance obtained from centipedes, scorpions, spiders, and arrow-poison frogs, used in various crafting recipes.

## Acquisition
Can be obtained through the following animals:

* Centipede
* Scorpion
* Spider
* Arrow-Poison Frog

*

*
## Usage
Venom is used in various crafting recipes, including:

* Locust Pesticide
* Herbicide
* Aseptic
* Purified Poison
