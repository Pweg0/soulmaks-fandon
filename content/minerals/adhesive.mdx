# Adhesive

**Adhesive** is a mineral in *Soulmask*.

## Tip
* This item cannot be crafted.
* Adhesive can be looted from the Ancient Ruins Dungeons in the Wasteland, located to the far North East corner of the map near Mysterious Portal (Northern Wasteland II).
