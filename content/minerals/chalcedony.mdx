# Chalcedony

**Chalcedony** is a mineral in *Soulmask*.
## Acquisition
It can be found as chest loot in Holy Ruins, Ancient Ruins Dungeons, and Dungeons.  It can also be crafted in a powered refining furnace.

Crafting: 5 crystal/5 ice/2 jade/10 phosphorous powder
## Usage
 is used to craft the following items:
*Steel Necklace
*Steel Ring
*Armor Enhancement
*Legguard Enhancement
*Shoes Enhancement
*Gloves Enhancement
*Necklace Enhancement
*Ring Enhancement
