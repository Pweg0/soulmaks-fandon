# Clay

thumb|257x257px
**Clay** é um mineral em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
Clay is a versatile mineral in Soulmask, essential for crafting and construction. Clay veins can be found near rivers and lakes and are mined with a pickaxe. It is Usado em various crafting recipes, including Ceramic Mold, Plaster, Well, Potting Table, Brewing Tank, Dye Vat, and Fertilizer Bucket. Along with many more
