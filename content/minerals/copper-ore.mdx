# Copper Ore

thumb|260x260px

**Copper Ore** is a mineral in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Copper Ore is a mineral in Soulmask. Copper Ore veins can be found in pits located across the map and mined with a Beast Bone Pickaxe or a pickaxe of a higher tier. It is used in various crafting recipes, including Copper Ingot, Calalite, and Blue Paint.

## Acquisition
Copper Ore veins can be found in pits located across the map and mined with a Beast Bone Pickaxe or a pickaxe of a higher tier.
