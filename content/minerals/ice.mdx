# Ice

**Ice** is an item in ''Soulmask.

## Obtenção
Ice can be mined with a pickaxe from small blue ice chunks found floating on and surrounding lakes in the Snow Mountain region.
## Uso
Ice is primarily used to power Ice Cellars. They have a decay timer of 2 hours while in the inventory, but one piece can power an Ice Cellar for 10 hours.

It can also be applied to armor for a temporary Heat Proof buff. Each piece adds 10 Heat Proof, up to a max of 50.
