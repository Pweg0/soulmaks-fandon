# Mysterious Crystal

**Mysterious Crystal** é um item em *Soulmask*.

It can be found as loot in chests in the Frost Canyon Ruínas Sagradas dungeon.

It is Usado em crafting Ancient Enhance Table, Necklace Enhancement, and Ring Enhancement.
