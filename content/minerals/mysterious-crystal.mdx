# Mysterious Crystal

**Mysterious Crystal** is an item in *Soulmask*.

It can be found as loot in chests in the Frost Canyon Holy Ruins dungeon.

It is used in crafting Ancient Enhance Table, Necklace Enhancement, and Ring Enhancement.
