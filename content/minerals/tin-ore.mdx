# Tin Ore

thumb|260x260px

**Tin Ore** is a mineral in *Soulmask*.

## Description
Tin Ore is a valuable mineral in Soulmask. It can be found in pits scattered throughout the map and requires a Beast Bone Pickaxe or a higher-tier pickaxe to mine. Tin Ore can be smelted into Tin Ingot in the Furnace.

## Acquisition
Tin Ore veins can be found in pits located across the map and mined with a Beast Bone Pickaxe or a pickaxe of a higher tier. 

## Usage
It can be smelted into Tin Ingot in the Furnace.
