# Bug Net

}}
**Bug Net** é uma ferramenta em *Soulmask*.

## Uso
This can be used to catch Firefly.

To catch a firefly, wait until it is dark outside. Move to a body of water such as a river, ocean, or lake. You'll know if you're approximately in the right area if you see glowing dots in the sky.

Crouch and move quietly toward a group of fireflies. Pull out the Bug Net and swing.
