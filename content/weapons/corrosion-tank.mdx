# Corrosion Tank

<br/><br/><br/>







}}
**Corrosion Tank** is a weapon in *Soulmask*.

## Tips
This can be used to apply a debuff on metal enemies, such as the Slayer and Mechanical Core you encounter in Dungeons, Ancient Ruins Dungeons, and Holy Ruins. This debuff allows you to do extra damage to those types of enemies.
