# Quaking Star

\}\}
**Quaking Star** is a throwing weapon in *Soulmask*.

When thrown it does not cause damage, but sticks to whatever it was thrown at with a glowing red crystal. This can be used to mark specific inimigos before a fight, or to indicate corridors already taken in a Masmorra maze.
