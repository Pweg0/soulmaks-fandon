# Ancient Enhance Table

}}
**Ancient Enhance Table** é uma construção em *Soulmask*.

## Descrição
An ancient technology only obtainable by reading texts about long-forgotten armor enhancing techniques.
## Obtenção
The  node in the Knowledge and Technology tree (Steel Pit Bonfire era) is not accessible until reading the Ancient Texts item, which can be looted after defeating the Central Core in the Frost Canyon Ruínas Sagradas.

Once the knowledge is unlocked, it Pode ser fabricado at a Building Workshop using the following materials:
*20x Mysterious Alloy 
*20x Mysterious Crystal 
*20x Mysterious Magnet 
*100x Blackstone Brick 
*30x Precision Part 
*30x Glass 
Rare material Locations:
*Mysterious Alloy is only Encontrado em Ruínas Sagradas or Ancient Dungeons
*Mysterious Magnet is only Encontrado em the Giant Crater (desert) Ruínas Sagradas
*Mysterious Crystal is only Encontrado em the Frost Canyon Ruínas Sagradas
## Uso
's primary use is to upgrade Steel armor pieces.
